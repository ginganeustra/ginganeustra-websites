New lead · Europe

Carney is being handed Europe’s parliamentary microphone.

Mark Carney will attend Ursula von der Leyen’s State of the Union address on Sept. 16 and address the European Parliament on Sept. 17. Foreign leaders have spoken there before — including Justin Trudeau and Volodymyr Zelenskyy — but European reporting says Carney will be the first non-European head of state or government to attend the EU’s annual State of the Union address in person.

The appearance comes just weeks before the Oct. 29-30 EU-Canada summit in Canada and days after Carney called for a much stronger and deeper economic and security partnership with Europe.

Story 2 · The precedent

Foreign leaders have addressed Parliament before — but not in quite this configuration.

Justin Trudeau addressed the European Parliament in March 2022 after Russia’s invasion of Ukraine. Volodymyr Zelenskyy spoke remotely in 2022 and in person in 2023. King Abdullah II of Jordan has addressed the plenary six times, most recently in 2025. Colombian President Iván Duque spoke at a formal sitting in Strasbourg in 2022.

The novelty is Carney’s presence for the State of the Union itself — the annual speech, established in 2010, in which the European Commission president sets priorities for the coming year — followed by Carney’s own address the next day.

Story 3 · Could Europe return the gesture?

Von der Leyen has already addressed Canada’s Parliament. Could she do it again in October?

There is no public confirmation of another parliamentary address during the Oct. 29-30 EU-Canada summit. But there is a striking precedent: von der Leyen addressed both houses of Parliament in Ottawa on March 7, 2023. The House of Commons record called it her first address to Canada’s Parliament and her first official visit to Canada.

A second joint address would therefore be significant rather than routine. For now, Canada at War is treating it as something to watch, not something scheduled.

Story 4 · What is EFE?

The Spanish wire service helped surface the Strasbourg story.

EFE is Spain’s state-owned international news agency and one of the world’s largest Spanish-language wire services, with a large reporting network across Europe and internationally. It reported the unusual nature of Carney’s State of the Union attendance. Euronews has now separately reported the Sept. 17 European Parliament address and quoted Parliament President Roberta Metsola saying the visit would help deepen EU-Canada ties.
