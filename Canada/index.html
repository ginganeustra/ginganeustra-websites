Could Canada have European links on a par with Norway?

Norway shows what genuinely deep integration with Europe looks like: Single Market access and the free movement of goods, services, people and capital — balanced by common rules, financial contributions and obligations far beyond a free-trade agreement. For Canada, it provides a concrete benchmark for how far Carney’s European pivot could ultimately go.
