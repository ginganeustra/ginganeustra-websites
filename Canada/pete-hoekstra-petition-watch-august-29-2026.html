CANADA AT WAR
Petition Watch · Canada–U.S. relations

263,263 signatures—and counting.

Count checked Aug. 29, 2026 · 8:30 p.m. ET

House of Commons e-petition e-7531 calls on the federal government to declare U.S. Ambassador Pete Hoekstra persona non grata, raise concerns about his conduct with Washington and seek a parliamentary review of alleged interference in Canadian affairs. The official site lists 263,263 validated signatures. The 500-signature threshold required for an e-petition to be presented and receive a government response has already been surpassed. The petition remains open until Nov. 18, 2026. A petition is not a binding vote and does not itself remove an ambassador.
