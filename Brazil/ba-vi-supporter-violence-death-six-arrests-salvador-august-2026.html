Salvador · public safety · 24 August 2026

Police confirm a death and six arrests after Ba-Vi violence.

Investigators say one man was fatally stabbed and four others were attacked on derby day. A separate shooting death has not been linked to the same case.

Published 24 August 2026 · 15:00 ET / 16:00 BRT

Police in Salvador have confirmed that José Alexandre Souza Borges, 28, died after being stabbed near Avenida 29 de Março on Sunday, the day of the Ba-Vi derby. Four other people were attacked, according to reporting based on the police investigation.

Six suspects arrested

Police arrested six men and said officers recovered knives, brass knuckles, fireworks and homemade explosive devices. The suspects were taken to the homicide department and booked in connection with aggravated homicide, four attempted homicides, possession of explosives and public disorder under Brazil’s supporter statute. Those are allegations; responsibility will be determined through the legal process.

A separate death remains separate

A 19-year-old member of Bamor was shot and killed in another incident. Police had not, at the time of publication, linked that death to the fatal stabbing case. BraGinga is keeping the incidents distinct while the investigations continue.

The football match itself was completed, with Bahia beating Vitória 2–0. This report concerns the confirmed off-field violence and police action; BraGinga does not report games in progress.

Sources: ge’s police report, Aug. 24; Folha de S.Paulo, Aug. 24.
