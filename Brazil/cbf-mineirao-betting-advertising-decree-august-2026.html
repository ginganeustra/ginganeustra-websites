Mineirão’s betting-ad issue reaches Cruzeiro’s matchday.

A state decree and a CBF warning have created a compliance question around the stadium’s commercial inventory.

The CBF has alerted Cruzeiro that Minas Gerais Decree No. 49,279, which restricts betting-company advertising and sponsorship on state public property, can affect a competition organised at Mineirão. The warning concerns advertising boards, screens and hospitality areas used on matchdays.

The federation’s position, as reported by UOL, is that Cruzeiro must ensure the venue meets the competition’s rules; it does not establish a new sanction or a deadline. Cruzeiro has asked its legal department to assess the practical consequences and still expects to use Mineirão.

The immediate football context is Saturday’s Flamengo visit. The wider question is how a state restriction will coexist with the commercial agreements that are now routine in Brazilian football.
