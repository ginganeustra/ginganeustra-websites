Brasileirão · Round 25 final

Pedro Rocha completes Coritiba’s late comeback.

Monday, 31 August 2026

Published 31 August 2026, 23:33 ET / 1 September, 00:33 BRT

Coritiba beat Remo 3–2 at the Mangueirão after a second half that changed direction twice and ended with former Remo forward Pedro Rocha scoring in added time.

An own goal by Zé Welison put Coritiba ahead. Remo answered quickly: Zé Ricardo equalized and Yago Pikachu converted the rebound after his penalty had been saved.

Fabinho made it 2–2 in the 75th minute. With Remo pushing for a winner, Coritiba goalkeeper Pedro Morisco began the counterattack that ended with Rocha’s decisive goal.

What it changes

Coritiba reached 37 points from 25 matches and moved into seventh place, three points behind Bahia in fifth. Remo remain 19th with 23 points after 25 matches and have now spent 21 successive rounds in the relegation zone.

The result completed Round 25. Coritiba next host Mirassol on Sunday, while Remo welcome Flamengo.
