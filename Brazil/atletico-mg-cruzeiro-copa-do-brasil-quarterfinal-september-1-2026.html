Atlético-MG turn derby around to eliminate Cruzeiro.

Tuesday, 1 September 2026

Published 1 September 2026, 23:33 ET / 2 September, 00:33 BRT

Atlético Mineiro are through to the Copa do Brasil semi-finals after a 2–1 home victory over Cruzeiro completed a derby comeback at Arena MRV.

Kaio Jorge put Cruzeiro ahead from the penalty spot in the first half. The tie had been level after a 1–1 first leg, so Atlético needed a response rather than a draw.

The turning point came when Cruzeiro left-back Lucas Villalba was sent off in the second half. Victor Hugo then levelled for Atlético-MG before Fred struck late to send the home side through.

What it means

Atlético-MG are the first confirmed semi-finalists and will face either Grêmio or Internacional. Their quarter-final ends 3–2 on aggregate after the 1–1 first leg.

Cruzeiro’s Copa do Brasil campaign is over. The club return to league duty with 39 points from 25 Brasileirão matches; Atlético-MG have 36 from 24.
