CBF says new refereeing rules added two minutes and 35 seconds of live play

Brazil’s football federation says average Série A ball-in-play time increased from 52:54 to 55:29 after updated officiating rules.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By BraGinga News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET

Brazil’s football federation reported Monday that the average amount of live play in Série A has risen after updated officiating measures were introduced.

According to the CBF, the average increased from 52 minutes 54 seconds to 55 minutes 29 seconds, an improvement of two minutes 35 seconds per match. The figures come from the federation’s own comparison and should be understood as its assessment of the policy’s early results.

The finding matters because the Brasileirão has long argued about delayed restarts, time-wasting and the amount of meaningful football hidden inside nominal 90-minute matches. A larger live-play window changes how teams manage leads and how officials calculate added time.

Monday’s finish at Beira-Rio illustrated why those margins matter. Remo’s equalizer arrived in the fifth minute of stoppage time, although that single incident should not be presented as proof of a broader statistical trend.

Sources: CBF: official refereeing report.
