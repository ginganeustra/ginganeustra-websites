Gabriel Jesus joins Barcelona through June 2029.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 11:36 ET / 12:36 BRT

Gabriel Jesus has joined Barcelona from Arsenal. The Catalan club announced the transfer on 1 September and confirmed a contract running to 30 June 2029.

The 29-year-old Brazilian leaves England for a new three-season commitment in Spain. Barcelona’s announcement does not disclose a transfer fee.

A new chapter after Arsenal

Reuters reports that Jesus leaves Arsenal with 32 goals in 123 appearances. Before moving to London, he scored 95 times in 236 Manchester City appearances and won four Premier League titles.

Barcelona’s official profile also recalls his beginnings at Palmeiras and his contribution to Brazil’s Olympic gold medal in 2016 and Copa América title in 2019.

For a forward whose career has already linked Brazilian development with two major English clubs, this is a confirmed change of league and setting. A Barcelona debut date was not included in the transfer announcement.

Sources: Barcelona’s official announcement, 1 September; Reuters, 1 September.
