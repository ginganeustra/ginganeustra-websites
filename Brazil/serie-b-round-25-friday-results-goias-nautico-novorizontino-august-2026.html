Goiás and Náutico win as Friday’s Série B round opens.

Friday, 28 August 2026

Published 28 August 2026, 23:34 ET / 00:34 BRT

Goiás 2–1 São Bernardo

Goiás defeated São Bernardo 2–1 at the Serrinha. Lucas Rodrigues put Goiás ahead, Pedrinho equalized for São Bernardo and Kadu Souza restored the home side’s lead. Goiás reached 36 points from 25 matches.

Náutico 2–1 Athletic

Náutico came from behind to beat Athletic 2–1 at the Aflitos. The result moved Náutico level with Athletic on 34 points after 25 matches.

Novorizontino 2–1 Sport

Novorizontino also won 2–1, defeating Sport in Novo Horizonte as Round 25 began.
