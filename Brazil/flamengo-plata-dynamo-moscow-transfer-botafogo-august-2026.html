Plata heads for Moscow as Flamengo prepare for Botafogo.

Published 28 August 2026, 18:55 ET · Updated 2 September 2026, 11:36 ET

Update, 2 September: This proposed transfer did not go through. Dynamo Moscow has confirmed it will not sign Plata. Read the confirmed cancellation report. The original report below records the agreement as it was reported in August.

Flamengo has agreed to sell Ecuador winger Gonzalo Plata to Dynamo Moscow for a reported €12 million, according to UOL. The report says only the final contract details remain and Plata is expected to travel to Russia.

The 25-year-old was left out of Flamengo’s plans for Sunday’s Brasileirão derby against Botafogo. He joined Flamengo in 2024 and scored nine goals in 100 appearances, according to UOL.

BraGinga is treating the club-to-club agreement as reported rather than a completed registration until either club publishes the final transfer announcement.

Sources: UOL, 28 August 2026; UOL analysis, 29 August 2026.
