Brasileirão · Round 24 · Sunday, Aug. 23

Palmeiras gets the chance Flamengo missed.

Flamengo’s 2–1 defeat at Cruzeiro leaves the leader with a clean opportunity to stretch the title gap when Vasco visits São Paulo.

Published Aug. 23, 2026 · 02:00 ET / 03:00 BRT

Saturday changed the pressure at the top. Flamengo could have drawn level with Palmeiras on points but conceded to Matheus Pereira in the 94th minute at Mineirão. Palmeiras therefore starts Sunday on 48 points, Flamengo on 45, with the leader able to move six clear if it beats Vasco.

Palmeiras–Vasco · 16:00 BRT

This is the title-race centrepiece. Palmeiras has not had to chase a Flamengo win overnight; instead it can punish the rival’s slip. Vasco arrives in the relegation zone and with its own off-field financial and stadium disputes adding pressure. The CBF also asked Palmeiras not to debut its new third shirt because of technical issues tied to semi-automated offside technology.

Bragantino–Grêmio · 16:00 BRT

Bragantino has games in hand and sits in the upper half; Grêmio is trying to create distance from the bottom four. This is one of the day’s most important table-compression games.

Vitória–Bahia · 16:00 BRT

The Ba-Vi gives the round its derby edge. Bahia is pushing toward the continental places, while Vitória enters from the lower half with little margin to drift toward the relegation fight.

Santos–Mirassol · 18:30 BRT

Mirassol is coming off Libertadores elimination on penalties at LDU. Santos can use that turnaround and home field to move away from the lower reaches of the table.

Chapecoense–São Paulo · 18:30 BRT

São Paulo has games in hand and needs domestic points; Chapecoense is rooted at the bottom. Anything short of a São Paulo win would deepen the sense of a missed opportunity.

Coritiba–Corinthians · 19:30 BRT

Corinthians comes out of a successful Libertadores week but into a domestic match that matters for its league position. Coritiba is only one point behind in the current table.

Botafogo–Athletico-PR closes Round 24 on Monday at 20:00 BRT.
