Libertadores + Sudamericana

Three advance. Two go home.

Corinthians, Santos and Vasco reach the quarter-finals; Mirassol and Botafogo exit.

BraGinga news desk · Published 20 August 2026, 23:00 ET / 21 August, 00:00 BRT

Corinthians 1–0 Rosario Central

Raniele was sent off in the 57th minute, but Memphis Depay returned from the bench and crossed for Breno Bidon to score the winner in the 79th. Corinthians faces Estudiantes next.

LDU 0–0 Mirassol — LDU wins 5–4 on penalties

The tie remained 1–1 after two legs. LDU converted all five shootout kicks; Victor Luís’s miss proved decisive.

Macará 0–0 Santos

Santos advanced 2–1 on aggregate. Gabriel Brazão made the key saves while Neymar and Gabriel Barbosa were rested. Atlético Mineiro awaits.

Olimpia 1–4 Vasco

Vasco responded to Esteban Matus’s opener through Thiago Mendes, David, Adson and Claudio Spinelli.

Botafogo 1–0 Cienciano

Danilo Pereira scored after seven minutes, but Cienciano advanced 6–2 on aggregate.
