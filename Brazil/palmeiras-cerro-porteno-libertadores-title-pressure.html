Palmeiras takes a 1–1 tie to Cerro Porteño while Flamengo closes in at home

Palmeiras visits Cerro Porteño on August 19 with its Libertadores tie level at 1–1 and its Brasileirão lead down to three points.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By BraGinga News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET

Palmeiras travels to Asunción on Wednesday with its Copa Libertadores round-of-16 tie against Cerro Porteño level at 1–1 and its domestic lead reduced to three points.

CONMEBOL lists the second leg for 7 p.m. local time at Ueno La Nueva Olla. The first leg in São Paulo also ended 1–1, leaving the quarter-final place open.

Saturday’s 3–2 defeat at Fluminense was especially costly because Palmeiras led twice. Flamengo then beat Mirassol 5–1, leaving Abel Ferreira’s side on 48 points compared with Flamengo’s 45 from one fewer match.

A positive result in Paraguay would stabilize the week, but it would not remove the domestic concern: Palmeiras must host relegation-threatened Vasco next while Flamengo also has the opportunity to gain ground.
