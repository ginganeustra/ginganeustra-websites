Botafogo · injury watch · Brasileirão

Fifteen stitches, one likely absence and a difficult Monday reset.

Warleson’s chin injury adds to Botafogo’s problems after continental elimination.

BraGinga news desk · Published 21 August 2026, 11:00 ET / 12:00 BRT

Botafogo goalkeeper Warleson required 15 stitches to his chin after a collision with Cienciano forward Carlos Hohberg during Thursday’s Copa Sudamericana match at Nilton Santos.

He left in the first half and Gabriel Batista replaced him. Botafogo will monitor the wound as it heals. The club has not formally ruled Warleson out of Monday’s Brasileirão match against Athletico-PR, but the current expectation reported by ge is that he will not play.

A depleted league return

The goalkeeping decision matters because Botafogo has only the Brasileirão left after a 6–2 aggregate elimination by Cienciano. The 1–0 home win did not come close to overturning the first-leg deficit.

Confirmed: 15 stitches and continued medical monitoring.
Expected, not confirmed: Warleson misses Monday’s match.

Why precision matters

An injury report is not a lineup announcement. BraGinga will update Warleson’s status only when Botafogo confirms the matchday decision or new medical information changes the outlook.
