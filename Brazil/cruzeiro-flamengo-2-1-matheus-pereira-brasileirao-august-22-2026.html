Brasileirão · Round 24 · Saturday

Cruzeiro beats Flamengo 2–1 with a last-kick Matheus Pereira winner

Matheus Pereira scored in the 94th minute as Cruzeiro came from behind to beat Flamengo, while Fluminense won and Internacional drew.

By BraGinga News Desk · Published 23:00 ET / 00:00 BRT
Cruzeiro 2–1 Flamengo
Fluminense 2–1 Remo
Internacional 0–0 Atlético-MG

Flamengo led at Mineirão through Pedro in the 21st minute, but Keny Arroyo equalized after halftime and Matheus Pereira completed the comeback in the 94th minute. The winner took a deflection on its way past Agustín Rossi.

The defeat leaves Flamengo on 45 points, three behind Palmeiras. Cruzeiro moves to 39 points in fifth place after its fourth consecutive league victory.

Fluminense turns it around

Remo struck first at the Maracanã through Jajá. Hulk brought Fluminense level, and Kevin Serna delivered the winner. Fluminense reaches 41 points and fourth place; Remo remains in the relegation zone on 23.

Inter remains stuck

Internacional and Atlético-MG drew 0–0 in Porto Alegre. Internacional has now gone eight league matches without a win and sits 16th on 25 points. Atlético-MG is seventh on 33.

What Saturday changed

The title gap remains three points instead of disappearing. Fluminense draws level with Athletico-PR on points, Cruzeiro closes to within two, and Internacional remains immediately above the relegation places.
