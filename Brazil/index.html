Brasileirão · Maracanã

Cano in stoppage time: Fluminense stun Palmeiras 3–2

Fluminense ended their post-World Cup winless run in dramatic fashion, beating Brasileirão leaders Palmeiras 3–2 at the Maracanã in Marcão’s first league match after Luis Zubeldía’s dismissal.

Palmeiras led twice, first through Gustavo Gómez and then through Mauricio. Hulk equalized before halftime, Kevin Serna made it 2–2 late in the second half, and Germán Cano supplied the stoppage-time winner.

The result leaves Palmeiras on 48 points. Flamengo remain six behind with two fewer matches played and visit Mirassol on Sunday, so the title race can tighten quickly. Fluminense rise to 38 points.

John Arias was loudly booed on his return to the Maracanã in Palmeiras colours.

Sources: UOL; Folha

New and developing — four new stories

Fluminense · dressing room

Riquelme omitted after Zubeldía reaction

Fluminense left 19-year-old Riquelme Felipe out of the Palmeiras squad after teammates interpreted his prayer-hands response to the coach’s departure as celebration. The club has not called the omission a formal punishment, but the episode turns a managerial reset into a test of dressing-room authority for Marcão.

UOL

Flamengo · market

Flamengo lowers the transfer temperature

After unsuccessful pursuits of Thiago Almada and Luiz Henrique, football director José Boto has reduced public market talk and Leonardo Jardim has emphasized the options already in his squad. Flamengo still want additions in attacking areas, but Sunday’s trip to Mirassol and Wednesday’s Libertadores decider now take priority. Bruno Henrique is a doubt with ankle pain.

UOL

Santos · Racing

Adonis Frías loan moves close

Santos and Racing Club are finalizing a proposed one-year loan for Argentine centre-back Adonis Frías, with an option to buy. If completed, he will not be selected for Sunday’s pressure match at Vasco. The possible exit compounds Santos’ defensive shortage: Lucas Veríssimo is injured and Cuca has tested midfielder Willian Arão at centre-back.

Seleção · development

CBF defines an “ideal player” model for the next cycle

The CBF is preparing a methodological guide describing the technical, tactical, competitive and behavioural profile sought for each position. Rodrigo Caetano and Carlo Ancelotti’s staff plan to take it into club academies while linking the under-15, under-17 and under-20 sides more closely to the senior team. Roughly 50 players developed abroad are also being monitored.

UOL

Corinthians · continuing

Memphis settlement remains unresolved

Memphis Depay’s camp continues to discuss outstanding financial matters with Corinthians. Fernando Diniz has publicly insisted that the departure cannot become an excuse, keeping the issue relevant as the club moves from the Rosario first leg into Sunday’s league meeting with Cruzeiro.
