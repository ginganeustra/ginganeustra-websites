NEW · Vasco venue litigation

The stadium argument now has a legal deadline

Vasco has sought an injunction against Maracanã management, which is controlled by Flamengo and Fluminense, after its request to use the stadium for the Copa do Brasil quarter-final against Vitória was rejected.

The club alleges discriminatory treatment and says it has not received technical reports supporting the refusal. Maracanã management says the maintenance company recommended preserving a heavily used pitch and notes that Vasco has used the stadium six times since April 2025. São Januário remains the venue initially indicated by the CBF unless the dispute changes that.

Lead · Continental knockouts

Brazil sends three clubs into the Libertadores quarter-finals on a dramatic two-night run

Fluminense had to survive a stoppage-time equalizer, a red card and a shootout in Mendoza. Fábio saved two penalties as the Rio side eliminated Independiente Rivadavia. Twenty-four hours later Flamengo and Palmeiras both finished their jobs: Flamengo beat Cruzeiro 2–1 at the Maracanã, while Gustavo Gómez’s header gave Palmeiras a 1–0 victory at Cerro Porteño.

Atlético Mineiro’s path in the Sudamericana was less tidy. Bragantino twice led in Belo Horizonte, but a late Tomás Cuello goal made it 2–2 and protected Atlético’s 3–2 aggregate advantage.
