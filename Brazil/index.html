What Brazil is talking about

Brasileirão · Round 25 finals

Vasco hit three; São Paulo end four-month drought

Tchê Tchê, Lucas Freitas and David delivered Vasco’s 3–1 win. Gustavo Santana scored his first senior goal as São Paulo won again.

Rio derby · Sunday preview

Flamengo and Botafogo meet with both needing a reset.

Flamengo return to the Maracanã after a 2–1 league loss at Cruzeiro; Botafogo arrive after a 3–2 defeat to Athletico-PR. Kickoff is 16:00 BRT.

São Paulo derby · Sunday preview

Corinthians–Santos brings another pressure match.

Corinthians host Santos at 16:00 BRT. Santos are looking for a response after a 3–0 Copa do Brasil defeat to Palmeiras; reports expect Neymar to start.

Bragantino · refereeing

Mancini attacks the VAR penalty in São Paulo’s win.

Bragantino coach Vagner Mancini called the penalty that opened Saturday’s 2–1 São Paulo win a “disgrace”; the decision followed a lengthy video review.

Brazil abroad · market watch

Roma signal interest in Luiz Henrique.

UOL reports that Roma have indicated a proposal for the Brazilian forward at Zenit. A transfer has not been announced.

Brazil abroad · Market watch

De Zerbi confirms Richarlison wants to leave Tottenham

The coach says Richarlison must bring the club an economically acceptable solution. No move has been announced.

Corinthians · Injury watch

Yuri setback reshapes Corinthians’ derby plans

UOL reports that pain at the site of his grade-two right-thigh injury forced the striker back one stage in recovery. Hugo Souza and Matheus Bidu are expected to return.

Santos · Market watch

Santos return to the market after transfer ban is lifted

Santos says NR Sports supplied immediate funding to clear the obligation behind the FIFA restriction. Reports of an Arthur deal remain unconfirmed.

Goiás and Náutico win as Friday’s Série B round opens
