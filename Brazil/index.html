Title race · 16:00 BRT

Palmeiras can turn Flamengo's miss into daylight

Flamengo had a chance to draw level on points Saturday and instead lost 2–1 at Cruzeiro on Matheus Pereira's stoppage-time winner. Palmeiras now controls the weekend: a win over relegation-threatened Vasco would make the lead six points.

UOL, Aug. 23; Folha, Aug. 23.

Ba-Vi · 16:00 BRT

Vitória's home edge meets Bahia's push upward

Vitória comes in off a 1–0 win over Botafogo and has been harder to handle at the Barradão. Bahia is sixth and needs the derby points to strengthen its position in the upper part of the table.

UOL, Aug. 23.

18:30 BRT

São Paulo cannot treat Chapecoense like a free square
