What Brazil is talking about

Brasileirão · Monday final

Pedro Rocha completes Coritiba’s late comeback

Remo led 2–1, but Fabinho equalized and former Remo forward Pedro Rocha struck in added time to give Coritiba a 3–2 victory.

Brasileirão · Round 25 finals

Palmeiras held; Flamengo move within four

Palmeiras drew 1–1 at Mirassol after Flamengo beat Botafogo 3–0, leaving four points between the top two. Bahia won 3–2 against Internacional and Grêmio beat Chapecoense 3–1.

Palmeiras · Title race

Mirassol hold the leaders

Eduardo put Mirassol ahead before Mauricio earned Palmeiras a 1–1 draw. The leaders finish Sunday four points above Flamengo.

Bahia · Top five

Bahia win five-goal fight

A 3–2 victory over Internacional takes Bahia to 40 points and fifth. Internacional drop into the relegation places.

Flamengo · Title race

Samuel Lino scores twice as Flamengo pressure Palmeiras

Jorge Carrascal added the third in the 3–0 win over Botafogo. Flamengo finish the weekend four points behind Palmeiras with a match in hand.

Brasileirão · Six-goal draw

Athletico and Fluminense trade six in Curitiba

Viveros’ late penalty rescued a 3–3 draw after Hulk, Canobbio and Ignácio had scored for Fluminense.
