Brasileirão · Monday final

Vitor Bueno’s last-kick free kick earns Remo a 1–1 draw at Internacional

Internacional had almost escaped a relegation six-pointer with three points. Then, in the fifth minute of added time, Vitor Bueno struck a free kick that gave Remo a 1–1 draw and turned the final match of Brasileirão Round 23 into a warning for both clubs.

Johan Carbonero had put Internacional in front in the third minute at Beira-Rio. The advantage survived virtually the entire evening, but the home side could not find a second goal and eventually paid for leaving the outcome open.

Officiating watch

CBF says new refereeing rules added two minutes and 35 seconds of live play

Brazil’s football federation reported Monday that the average amount of live play in Série A has risen after updated officiating measures were introduced.

According to the CBF, the average increased from 52 minutes 54 seconds to 55 minutes 29 seconds, an improvement of two minutes 35 seconds per match. The figures come from the federation’s own comparison and should be understood as its assessment of the policy’s early results.
