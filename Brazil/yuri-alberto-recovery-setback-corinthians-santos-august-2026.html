Yuri setback reshapes Corinthians’ derby plans.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 10:55 ET / 11:55 BRT

Yuri Alberto’s attempted return has been delayed after renewed pain in the back of his right thigh, UOL reported Saturday morning.

The Corinthians striker began physical transition work on Wednesday after a grade-two muscle injury. According to UOL’s reporting, the discomfort forced the club to take him back one stage in the recovery plan. He is therefore unavailable for Sunday’s Brasileirão derby against Santos.

Yuri has not played since 30 July, when he was injured during the scoreless draw with Athletico-PR. The layoff kept him out of the Copa do Brasil ties with Internacional and the Libertadores round-of-16 matches against Rosario Central.

Two expected returns

The same report says goalkeeper Hugo Souza and left-back Matheus Bidu are cleared and expected to be available. Hugo has resumed training after a knee strain. Bidu, who had been playing with pubic discomfort, missed the defeat at Coritiba as part of load management.

Corinthians host Santos at Neo Química Arena on Sunday at 4 p.m. BRT, 3 p.m. Eastern. The injury information affects selection, but BraGinga will publish the match only after a confirmed final score.

Sources: UOL injury report, 29 August 2026; CBF competition calendar.
