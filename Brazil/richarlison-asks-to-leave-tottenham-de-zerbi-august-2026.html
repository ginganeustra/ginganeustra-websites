Richarlison wants to leave Tottenham.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 18:01 ET / 19:01 BRT

Tottenham coach Roberto De Zerbi says Richarlison has repeatedly asked to leave the club.

De Zerbi made the disclosure after Tottenham’s 2–0 loss to Newcastle on Saturday. Richarlison was not selected for the match. The coach also said defenders Kevin Danso and Pape Matar Sarr had asked to go.

That does not make Richarlison a free agent. De Zerbi said the players must bring Tottenham an economically acceptable solution if they are to leave. The Brazil forward remains under contract until June 2027.

Confirmed request, unconfirmed destination

No buying club, fee or agreement has been announced. Reports connecting Richarlison with possible destinations therefore remain separate from the confirmed fact that he wants an exit.

The timing makes the decision urgent: the English transfer window is close to shutting. Richarlison joined Tottenham from Everton in 2022 after earlier Premier League service with Watford.

Sources: ge report and De Zerbi remarks, 29 August 2026; UOL report, 29 August 2026.
