Wallace Yan joins Bragantino through August 2031.

Thursday, 3 September 2026

Published 3 September 2026, 11:37 ET / 12:37 BRT

Red Bull Bragantino have signed Wallace Yan from Flamengo on a contract through August 2031. Both clubs confirmed the permanent transfer on Wednesday, 2 September.

The 21-year-old attacking midfielder moves to Bragança Paulista after coming through Flamengo’s youth system and joining their senior squad in 2024. Bragantino’s announcement recalls an earlier attempt to sign him at the start of this season that did not reach completion.

In his presentation interview, Wallace Yan said he would have to earn his place in the new squad. He said he had been training normally at Flamengo and hoped to make his Bragantino debut the following week; that was the player’s expectation, not a confirmed selection.

CNN Brasil reported a transfer fee of €6 million. Flamengo’s announcement confirms the permanent move without stating a fee.

Flamengo have also confirmed Erick Pulgar’s contract renewal through 2029.

Sources: Bragantino’s signing announcement; Flamengo’s transfer statement; CNN Brasil’s transfer report.
