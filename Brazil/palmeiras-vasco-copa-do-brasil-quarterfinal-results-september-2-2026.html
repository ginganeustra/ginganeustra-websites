Palmeiras and Vasco reach the Copa do Brasil semi-finals.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 23:37 ET / 3 September, 00:37 BRT

Palmeiras and Vasco secured their Copa do Brasil semi-final places on Wednesday, 2 September, without conceding in their away second legs.

Palmeiras drew 0–0 with Santos at Vila Belmiro. The club’s official report confirms that the 3–0 first-leg victory was enough to take them through. Santos could not make an opening in the aggregate deficit.

Vasco won 2–0 at Vitória’s Barradão, following their 1–0 home win. Andrés Gómez and Puma Rodríguez scored in the second half, according to UOL and Gazeta Esportiva’s completed-match reports.

The two results set up a Palmeiras–Vasco semi-final. Each side advanced 3–0 on aggregate, but by different routes: Palmeiras protected the advantage already established at home, while Vasco added two more away goals.

These were cup fixtures and do not add points to the Brasileirão table. Atlético-MG had already advanced on Tuesday after beating Cruzeiro 2–1.

Sources: Palmeiras’ official report; UOL on Vitória–Vasco; Gazeta Esportiva’s final match summary.
