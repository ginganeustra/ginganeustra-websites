Fluminense makes Marcão permanent for the rest of 2026

The club will not enter the coaching market. Marcão and Fluminense’s permanent staff have the first team through the end of the season.

Published 23 August 2026, 11:00 ET / 12:00 BRT

Fluminense has confirmed Marcão as its permanent first-team coach for the rest of the 2026 season, ending the immediate search for a successor to Luis Zubeldía.

Club president Mattheus Montenegro announced the decision after Saturday’s 2–1 comeback victory over Remo. He said Fluminense trusts Marcão and the permanent coaching staff and will not look outside the club for another manager.

Marcão’s latest spell began with three consequential results: a 3–2 league win over Palmeiras, Copa Libertadores progress against Independiente Rivadavia on penalties, and the 2–1 victory over Remo.

A familiar figure gets the job

Marcão has worked on Fluminense’s permanent staff since 2013 and has repeatedly taken interim control. This time the appointment is no longer temporary: he is responsible for the senior side through the end of the campaign.

The Remo win kept Fluminense fourth in the Brasileirão on 41 points. Their next league match is away to Athletico-PR on 30 August.

Why it matters

The decision gives the squad continuity during a demanding domestic and continental schedule. It also makes a clear bet on a coach who already knows the club, its players and its internal structure rather than beginning another external search.
