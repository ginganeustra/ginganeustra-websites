Dynamo cancel Plata and Matheus Martins transfers.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 11:36 ET / 12:36 BRT

Dynamo Moscow have confirmed they will not sign Gonzalo Plata from Flamengo or Matheus Martins from Botafogo. Both players are returning to their Brazilian clubs.

The Russian club’s statement says it decided against the transfers for several reasons connected to its long-term interests. It does not identify a specific medical condition.

Flamengo dispute the medical assessment

In a statement reproduced by UOL, Flamengo said Plata was fit for elite football and rejected the suggestion of a serious chronic problem. The club said it would seek appropriate remedies over the handling of the proposed move.

The distinction matters: the transfers have collapsed, but the clubs’ conflicting medical positions are not an independently established diagnosis.

The reversal supersedes BraGinga’s earlier report of an agreement. Plata’s departure can no longer be counted as completed business.

Sources: Dynamo Moscow’s official statement, 2 September; Flamengo’s response, reproduced by UOL, 1 September.
