Benfica signs Alan Santos from Flamengo.

The 19-year-old winger has completed a permanent move to Lisbon, confirmed independently by both clubs.

Published 24 August 2026 · 15:00 ET / 16:00 BRT

Benfica has signed Alan Santos permanently from Flamengo. The Portuguese club describes the 19-year-old as a winger and confirmed the completed transfer on Monday.

Two-club confirmation

Flamengo announced that it had reached an agreement to transfer the academy player to Benfica. Benfica then published its own confirmation, identifying Alan Santos as a permanent addition from the Rio club.

The development path

Born on 10 May 2007, Alan Santos moves from Flamengo’s academy structure into Benfica’s development system. Neither official announcement published a transfer fee, so BraGinga is not presenting one as confirmed.

Sources: Flamengo’s official announcement, Aug. 24; Benfica’s official announcement, Aug. 24.
