Flamengo add two forwards as Palmeiras secure Flaco López.

Tuesday, 1 September 2026

Published 1 September 2026, 11:35 ET / 12:35 BRT

Brazil’s top two clubs made three significant attacking announcements on Tuesday morning. Flamengo confirmed the arrivals of Anthony Valencia and Joaquín Freitas, while Palmeiras extended Flaco López’s contract through the end of 2030.

Flamengo add two different profiles

Anthony Valencia, a 23-year-old Ecuador international, joined Flamengo from Royal Antwerp and signed through 2031. The left-footed forward will wear No. 14. Flamengo says he scored 13 goals and supplied four assists during his four years in Belgium after beginning his senior career with Independiente del Valle.

Joaquín Freitas gives Flamengo a younger development option. The 19-year-old Argentine forward signed a five-year agreement and will wear No. 30. He began with Acassuso, made his professional debut at 16 and moved to River Plate in 2024. Flamengo’s announcement confirms the transfer that Argentine reports had described as close over the weekend; the club did not disclose a fee.

The timing matters. Flamengo are four points behind Brasileirão leaders Palmeiras with a match in hand. Neither signing announcement included a debut date.

Palmeiras retain their leading scorer

Palmeiras’ response was continuity. López has signed through 2030 after reaching 21 goals in 45 appearances this season. The club lists 78 goals in 225 appearances since the Argentine arrived in 2022.

The renewal protects a proven source of goals during a title race in which Palmeiras lead Flamengo by four points. López is also now the club’s leading scorer in 2026 and its third-highest scorer of this century, behind Raphael Veiga and Dudu.

Sources: Flamengo on Anthony Valencia; Flamengo on Joaquín Freitas; Palmeiras on Flaco López’s renewal.
