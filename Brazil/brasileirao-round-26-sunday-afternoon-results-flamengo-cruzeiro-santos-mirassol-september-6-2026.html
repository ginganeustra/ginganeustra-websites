Flamengo take lead; Cruzeiro, Santos and Mirassol win

Sunday, 6 September 2026

Published 6 September 2026, 18:00 ET / 19:00 BRT

Archive photograph: Estádio Olímpico do Pará, Jorge Andrade / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0.

Samuel Lino scored the only goal as Flamengo beat Remo 1–0 in Belém and moved to 54 points. The result puts Flamengo two points above Palmeiras, who had not completed their Sunday match when this edition was published.

Joaquín Freitas and Anthony Valencia made their Flamengo debuts. Remo remain on 23 points after a sixth league match without a win.

Cruzeiro close on the top four

Cruzeiro beat Athletico-PR 3–1 at the Mineirão. Matheus Pereira opened the scoring, João Cruz equalized in first-half stoppage time, and Lucas Romero and Kaio Jorge settled the match after the break. Cruzeiro rise to 42 points; Athletico remain third on 45.

Santos survive Inter comeback

Gabigol scored twice and Cristian Oliva added another as Santos won 3–2 at Internacional. Inter played most of the match with ten men after Matheus Bahia was sent off, but late goals from Aguirre and Bruno Henrique tightened the finish. Santos move to 32 points; Internacional remain in the relegation places on 25.

Mirassol turn it around

Mirassol came from behind to beat Coritiba 2–1. Pedro Rocha put the hosts ahead, Gustavo Silva levelled three minutes later and Bruno Santos scored the late winner. A Coritiba penalty was overturned after a VAR review in the final passage of play.
