Weekend guide · Brasileirão Round 24

Three Saturday games, then a Sunday that can move every race.

Round 24 begins after an exhausting continental week, putting rotation, recovery and league priorities in the same frame.

Saturday’s schedule is Fluminense–Remo (4 p.m. BRT), Internacional–Atlético Mineiro (6:30 p.m.) and Cruzeiro–Flamengo (8:30 p.m.). Sunday follows with Palmeiras–Vasco, Bragantino–Grêmio, Vitória–Bahia and Santos–Mirassol at 4 p.m.; Chapecoense–São Paulo at 4:30 p.m.; Botafogo–Athletico-PR at 6:30 p.m.; and Coritiba–Corinthians at 8:30 p.m.

Where the weekend turns

Palmeiras begins the round on 48 points; Flamengo has 45 with a game in hand. Flamengo’s visit to Cruzeiro comes only days after their Libertadores tie, while Palmeiras hosts Vasco on Sunday. That is the clearest title-race comparison of the round: one contender plays first, the other with the result already known.

Fluminense–Remo also has two sharply different urgencies. Fluminense returns to league duty after its Libertadores exit, while Remo starts the round in the relegation places. Internacional, 16th before the round, hosts Atlético Mineiro with little margin for another dropped home result.

Derby and continental pressure

Vitória–Bahia is the Ba-Vi, a fixture that does not need the table to create tension. Santos–Mirassol and Botafogo–Athletico-PR matter at both ends of a tightly packed middle; Coritiba–Corinthians closes the schedule with Corinthians carrying the after-effects of its Libertadores advancement.
