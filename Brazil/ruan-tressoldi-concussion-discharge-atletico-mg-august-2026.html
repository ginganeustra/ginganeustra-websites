Ruan discharged after concussion in the Mineirão cup tie.

Scans found no structural injury to Ruan Tressoldi’s skull or spine. The Atlético-MG defender now enters the CBF’s graduated return-to-play protocol.

Published 26 August 2026 · 12:00 ET / 13:00 BRT

Atlético-MG defender Ruan Tressoldi has been discharged from hospital after suffering a concussion in Tuesday night’s 1–1 Copa do Brasil quarter-final draw with Cruzeiro.

According to Atlético-MG information reported by UOL, imaging found no structural injury to his skull or spine. Ruan remains under the club’s medical care and must complete the Brazilian federation’s concussion protocol before he can be cleared.

Ruan left the match after landing on his head during an aerial challenge with Cruzeiro forward Kaio Jorge. He initially tried to continue, but was replaced after showing symptoms and was taken to hospital for examination.

What the protocol means

CBF rules require a minimum six-day period away from matches after the injury, followed by a staged return only while the player remains symptom-free. His availability for the September 1 second leg at Arena MRV will depend on clinical progress and medical clearance.

The football context

Cruzeiro and Atlético-MG are level at 1–1 after the first leg. A win in the return sends either club to the semi-finals; another draw sends the tie to penalties.

Sources: UOL’s hospital-discharge report, 26 August 2026; ge’s match incident report, 25 August 2026.
