Botafogo · confirmed manager change

Franclim Carvalho is out. Rodrigo Bellão takes interim charge.

Bellão’s third first-team spell of 2026 begins with Botafogo facing a 6–1 deficit against Cienciano.

BraGinga news desk · Published 20 August 2026, 15:00 ET

Botafogo dismissed head coach Franclim Carvalho on Tuesday and placed Rodrigo Bellão in interim charge. Bellão, normally attached to the under-20 side, will lead the first team in Thursday’s Copa Sudamericana return against Cienciano at Nilton Santos.

The immediate equation: Botafogo lost the first leg 6–1 in Cusco. A five-goal win is required to force penalties; a six-goal win would complete the comeback in regulation.

A third interim spell

This is Bellão’s third turn with the senior side in 2026. He led the young team used at the start of the Campeonato Carioca and later covered three matches after Martín Anselmi’s departure.

Why it matters beyond one match

Carvalho’s exit is the latest coaching change in a volatile Série A season. It also joins football operations to Botafogo’s larger governance story while the club’s control and financing remain contested.
