CBF sends specialist to inspect India friendly pitch

A preliminary visit raised concerns about grass density and injury risk, so the CBF is sending its own pitch specialist to Kolkata.

Published 23 August 2026, 11:00 ET / 12:00 BRT

The CBF will send agronomist Maristela Kuhn to inspect the Salt Lake Stadium pitch in Kolkata before Brazil’s friendly against India on 3 October.

A preliminary visit by national-team supervisor Sérgio Dimas and physiologist Guilherme Passos raised concern about the density of the grass and the potential injury risk. The CBF has not indicated that the match itself is in doubt.

Brazil–India remains scheduled for 3 October at Salt Lake Stadium. The next step is a technical inspection of the playing surface.

What the inspection can change

Kuhn is expected to assess the pitch directly and recommend any work needed before the match. Possible measures reported in Brazil include soil decompaction, additional seeding and deeper irrigation.

The trip is a preventive move: it gives the host venue time to act before Brazil travels and gives the national team a clearer assessment of the surface.

Why the CBF is acting now

Pitch condition has a direct effect on player safety and match quality. By sending a specialist weeks in advance, the federation can document the condition and follow any remediation rather than relying only on a match-week inspection.
