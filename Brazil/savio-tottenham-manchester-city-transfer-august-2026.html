Sávio joins Tottenham from Manchester City.

The 22-year-old Brazil winger leaves City after two seasons and joins Roberto De Zerbi’s rebuild in north London.

BraGinga news desk · Published 25 August 2026, 11:00 ET / 12:00 BRT

Brazil winger Sávio has completed a permanent transfer from Manchester City to Tottenham Hotspur, both clubs confirmed Tuesday.

Neither club disclosed the contract length or financial terms. Reuters reported that British media valued the deal at £75 million, with a possible further £10 million in add-ons.

What Tottenham is buying

Sávio, 22, made 84 appearances and scored seven goals across two seasons with Manchester City. Tottenham sporting director Johan Lange said the winger’s ability, style and mentality fit the squad being built under head coach Roberto De Zerbi.

De Zerbi highlighted Sávio’s “energy, imagination and quality” in wide positions. The player arrives after telling Tottenham that the head coach’s personal approach and promise to help him reach another level influenced the move.

Confirmed: the transfer is permanent. Tottenham and Manchester City did not publish the fee or the length of Sávio’s contract in their announcements.

The Brazil angle

Born in São Mateus, Espírito Santo, Sávio began his senior career with Atlético Mineiro and broke through in Europe during Girona’s third-place 2023/24 La Liga season. Tottenham says he has 13 senior Brazil caps and one Seleção goal.

The move gives one of Brazil’s established wide options a prominent role in Tottenham’s rebuild. Club form will shape his next Seleção case, but selection remains a decision for Brazil’s coaching staff.

Sources: Tottenham Hotspur — official transfer announcement; Manchester City — official departure announcement; Reuters — reported fee and transfer context.
