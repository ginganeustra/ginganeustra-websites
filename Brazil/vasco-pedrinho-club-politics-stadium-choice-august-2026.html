Vasco · Copa do Brasil · venue dispute

The Maracanã argument is now a court case.

Vasco seeks an injunction after the stadium’s Flamengo-Fluminense management group rejected its request to host Vitória.

BraGinga news desk · Published 20 August 2026, 11:00 ET · Updated 21 August 2026, 15:00 ET / 16:00 BRT

Vasco has asked a Rio court for an injunction authorizing it to use the Maracanã for the first leg of its Copa do Brasil quarter-final against Vitória.

The move follows a refusal by the stadium management group controlled by Flamengo and Fluminense. The CBF had initially indicated São Januário as the venue, but Vasco sent a new request for the Maracanã on 19 August and wants the issue resolved before ticket sales begin.

Vasco’s argument

The club alleges discriminatory treatment and says the refusal was not supported by technical reports demonstrating why the pitch could not accommodate the match.

The stadium manager’s answer

Maracanã management says the company responsible for maintaining the surface recommended preserving it. It also says Vasco has been permitted to use the stadium six times since April 2025. The pitch has carried a heavy schedule and has faced criticism over its condition.

Current status: Vasco has sought an injunction. São Januário remains the venue initially indicated by the CBF unless the legal or administrative position changes.

More than a stadium choice

The argument now tests access to Rio’s largest football venue, relations among three major clubs and the time needed to organize a national cup match. BraGinga will update the file when the court rules or the venue is officially confirmed.
