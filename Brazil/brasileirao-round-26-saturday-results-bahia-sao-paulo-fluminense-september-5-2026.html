Bahia surge into top five; São Paulo and Fluminense win

Saturday, 5 September 2026

Published 5 September 2026, 22:57 ET / 23:57 BRT

Archive photograph: Estádio Nabi Abi Chedid, Renato Patinhas / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0.

Bahia turned Saturday’s opening match around to beat Red Bull Bragantino 3–2 in Bragança Paulista. It was Bahia’s fifth comeback victory of the 2026 Brasileirão—the most in the division—and extended their unbeaten league run to ten matches.

The win lifts Bahia to 43 points and fifth place after 26 matches. Bragantino remain ninth on 35.

São Paulo strike twice in two minutes

São Paulo beat Atlético-MG 2–0 through second-half goals by Luciano and Iago Borduchi. The goals arrived two minutes apart, and the victory takes São Paulo to 33 points from 25 matches.

Luciano’s goal was his 116th for São Paulo, moving him past Careca on the club’s all-time scoring list. Atlético-MG remain on 36 points.

Acosta decides the Rio derby

Lucho Acosta scored in the 60th minute as Fluminense defeated Vasco 1–0. Fluminense climb to 45 points and fourth place; Vasco stay on 25 points, just above the relegation positions.
