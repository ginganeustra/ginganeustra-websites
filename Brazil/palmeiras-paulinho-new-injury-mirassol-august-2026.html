Paulinho setback removes Palmeiras option at Mirassol

Published and updated 28 August 2026, 19:15 ET / 20:15 BRT

Palmeiras forward Paulinho has suffered a new injury to the long adductor tendon in his right leg, ending the prospect of a return against Mirassol on Sunday.

The club had been managing his return from a previous right-leg operation. The new problem occurred during the transition back to full training, according to ge. The report says Palmeiras now expect him to return only after the next FIFA window, which runs into early October.

The leader travels to Mirassol with a six-point advantage at the top of Série A, but without a player it had hoped could broaden the attacking choices for the run-in. The match is scheduled for 18:30 BRT Sunday.

Sources: ge report; CBF Série A table.
