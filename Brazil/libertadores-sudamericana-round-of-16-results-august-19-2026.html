Brazil’s continental night: three Libertadores survivors and an Atlético escape

Flamengo, Palmeiras and Fluminense are through in the Libertadores; Atlético Mineiro is through in the Sudamericana. Here is what happened and what it changes.

← Back to the BraGinga front page

Flamengo 2–1 Cruzeiro — Flamengo advances 3–2

Giorgian de Arrascaeta put Flamengo ahead at the Maracanã after work from Pedro. Lucas Romero equalized for Cruzeiro in the second half, but Samuel Lino restored Flamengo’s lead and sent the Rio club into the quarterfinals. The 1–1 first leg in Belo Horizonte meant Flamengo’s home win was enough for a 3–2 aggregate victory.

Cerro Porteño 0–1 Palmeiras — Palmeiras advances 2–1

Palmeiras went to Asunción with the tie level at 1–1 and found the decisive moment through captain Gustavo Gómez, whose headed goal gave Abel Ferreira’s team a 1–0 away victory and a 2–1 aggregate passage.

Independiente Rivadavia 1–1 Fluminense — Fluminense wins 5–4 on penalties

The Mendoza tie was pushed to the edge. Fluminense scored through Kevin Serna, then played part of the second half with 10 men after Agustín Canobbio was sent off. A stoppage-time penalty converted by Álex Arce forced the shootout. The shootout belonged to goalkeeper Fábio, who made two saves as Fluminense won 5–4.

Atlético Mineiro 2–2 Red Bull Bragantino — Atlético advances 3–2

Atlético entered with a 1–0 first-leg advantage. Bragantino twice led through Eric Ramires and Eduardo Sasha; Maycon equalized once and Tomás Cuello struck in stoppage time to make it 2–2. The draw protected Atlético’s aggregate advantage.

What comes Thursday

  • Libertadores: Corinthians hosts Rosario Central after a 0–0 first leg; LDU hosts Mirassol after a 1–1 first leg.
  • Sudamericana: Macará hosts Santos with Santos 2–1 ahead; Olimpia hosts Vasco after 0–0; Botafogo hosts Cienciano trailing 6–1.
