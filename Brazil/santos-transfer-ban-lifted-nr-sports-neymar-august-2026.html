Santos return to the market after transfer ban is lifted.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 09:15 ET / 10:15 BRT

Santos says it has paid the obligation that triggered its FIFA registration ban and is again eligible to operate in the player market.

In a club statement dated Thursday, Santos said the transaction was made possible by NR Sports, the company associated with Neymar’s family, which supplied an immediate injection of funds. In return, Santos and NR Sports will jointly negotiate currently unsold commercial inventory on the club’s shirt.

The club statement did not disclose the amount or identify the creditor. ESPN and UOL reported that the restriction arose from an approximately €2-million debt to Monaco connected with the 2023 transfer of midfielder Jean Lucas. Both outlets reported that clearing the obligation lifted the FIFA measure.

The distinction matters: this was a player-registration restriction, not a ban on Santos discussing potential deals. Its removal allows the club to register reinforcements, subject to the ordinary rules and deadlines of the Brazilian window.

What is confirmed — and what is not

Santos’ return to the market and NR Sports’ financial support are confirmed by the club. Reports linking free-agent midfielder Arthur Melo with Santos remain transfer reporting rather than a completed signing; BraGinga will treat any move as confirmed only after an official announcement.

The development lands before Sunday’s Brasileirão derby against Corinthians. Santos enter Round 25 in 14th place with 26 points from 23 matches.

Sources: Santos FC official statement, 27 August 2026; ESPN report, 28 August 2026; UOL report, 27 August 2026.
