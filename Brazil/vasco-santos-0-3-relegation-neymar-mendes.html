Relegation watch

Santos wins 3–0 at Vasco and changes the shape of the relegation battle

Santos climbed out of the relegation places by beating Vasco da Gama 3–0, leaving Vasco 19th in Brazil’s Série A.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By BraGinga News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET2 min read

Santos produced one of the most consequential results of Round 23 by beating Vasco da Gama 3–0 in Rio de Janeiro. The win lifted Santos to 25 points and 14th place; Vasco fell to 19th with 22.

The scale of the swing matters more than the score alone. Santos moved two points above the bottom four, while Vasco is now two points behind Internacional, the first club outside the relegation positions.

Ge described the defeat as an urgent warning for Vasco and coach Pedro Emanuel after a prolonged run without a league victory. The club’s problems are now visible both in the standings and in the gap between its attacking promise and its defensive results.

The fixture also drew attention to tension between Neymar and Thiago Mendes, but the lasting significance is sporting: a direct rival collected all three points while Vasco’s goal difference dropped to minus-11.
