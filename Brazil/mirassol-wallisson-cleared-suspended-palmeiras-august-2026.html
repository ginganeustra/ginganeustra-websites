BRAGINGA

Wallisson is cleared — but still out against Palmeiras.

Published 28 August 2026, 18:55 ET · Updated 29 August 2026, 09:15 ET

The Superior Tribunal of Sports Justice cleared Mirassol midfielder Wallisson of the disciplinary charge arising from his sending-off against Santos, ge reported Friday.

The acquittal does not erase the automatic one-match suspension attached to the red card. Wallisson therefore remains unavailable for Sunday’s home match against Brasileirão leader Palmeiras.

Ge also reported that Mirassol was fined R$3,000 for returning one minute late for the second half and for goalkeeping coach Alberto Oliveira’s conduct.
