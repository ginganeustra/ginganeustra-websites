Textor says he will return. Botafogo’s recovery plan says the fight is not over.

John Textor’s public intervention and Botafogo SAF’s revised judicial-recovery plan tell different versions of a live ownership crisis.

BraGinga · 20 August 2026 · 11:00 ET

John Textor says he is pursuing cases in England, the United States and Brazil to regain control of Eagle Bidco, which holds 90% of Botafogo SAF. That is his position in an active dispute, not a court ruling.

Botafogo SAF’s revised judicial-recovery proposal removed Eagle’s earlier protection as an exempt party. According to ge, that can leave Eagle exposed to potential claims. The plan still requires review by the court, the Public Prosecutor’s Office, the judicial administrator and creditors.

Why it matters

This is about control, creditor claims and the financial room in which football decisions are made—not merely a personality clash.

Reporting basis: ESPN Brasil, 20 August; ge, 17 August.
