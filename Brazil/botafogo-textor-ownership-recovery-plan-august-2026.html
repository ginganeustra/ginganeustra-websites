Textor says he will return. Botafogo’s recovery plan says the fight is not over.

John Textor says he is pursuing cases in England, the United States and Brazil to regain control of Eagle Bidco, which holds 90% of Botafogo SAF. His claim is part of a live dispute, not a court ruling.

Botafogo SAF’s revised judicial-recovery proposal removed Eagle’s prior protection as an exempt party. According to ge, that can leave Eagle exposed to potential claims. The proposal still requires review by the court, the Public Prosecutor’s Office, the judicial administrator and creditors.

Why it matters: this is about control, creditor claims and the financial room in which football decisions are made—not simply a personality clash.

Sources: ESPN Brasil, 20 August; ge, 17 August.
