The full Round 23 programme ended Monday with Internacional 1–1 Remo. The other results were Fluminense 3–2 Palmeiras, Mirassol 1–5 Flamengo, Corinthians 1–2 Cruzeiro, Vasco da Gama 0–3 Santos, Atlético Mineiro 3–0 Grêmio, Vitória 1–0 Botafogo, Athletico Paranaense 1–1 Red Bull Bragantino, São Paulo 1–1 Coritiba and Chapecoense 3–3 Bahia.

The next major domestic meeting is Cruzeiro against Flamengo on Saturday, while Palmeiras faces Vasco on Sunday. Before then, the Libertadores and Sudamericana second legs will occupy most of the country’s leading clubs.
