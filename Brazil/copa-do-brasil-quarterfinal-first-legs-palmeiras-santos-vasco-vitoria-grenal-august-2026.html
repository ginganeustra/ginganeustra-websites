Copa do Brasil · quarter-finals · first legs complete

Palmeiras take control; Vasco edge Vitória; GreNal stays level.

The three remaining first legs are finished: Palmeiras 3–0 Santos, Vasco 1–0 Vitória and Internacional 0–0 Grêmio.

Published 28 August 2026 · 01:10 ET / 02:10 BRT

The Copa do Brasil quarter-final picture is now complete after three first legs across Wednesday and Thursday.

Palmeiras 3–0 Santos

Palmeiras built the week's clearest advantage at the Nubank Parque. Flaco López scored twice and Andreas Pereira added the other as the league leaders dominated Santos. The return is Wednesday, Sept. 2, at Vila Belmiro.

Vasco 1–0 Vitória

Vasco protected home field at São Januário with a one-goal victory. The margin leaves the tie very much alive for the return in Salvador, but gives Vasco something tangible to defend after a difficult league stretch.

Internacional 0–0 Grêmio

GreNal 453 ended scoreless at the Beira-Rio. The match produced 43 fouls and controversy around a challenge by Carlos Vinícius on Bruno Gomes that did not lead to a red card. The second leg is Sept. 3 at Arena do Grêmio; another draw would send the tie to penalties.

Where the bracket stands

Cruzeiro and Atlético-MG are also level after their 1–1 first leg. Palmeiras carries a three-goal cushion, Vasco a one-goal cushion, and both Minas Gerais and Porto Alegre ties are level heading into the returns.

Sources: Federação Paulista de Futebol: Palmeiras 3–0 Santos, Aug. 26; Jornal do Brasil: Vasco 1–0 Vitória, Aug. 26; UOL: Internacional 0–0 Grêmio, Aug. 27.
