Parque São Jorge hears a demand for intervention.

Hundreds of supporters gathered at the club’s headquarters, calling for political change and judicial intervention.

BraGinga news desk · Published 22 August 2026, 15:00 ET / 16:00 BRT

Hundreds of Corinthians supporters gathered outside Parque São Jorge on Saturday morning to demand changes to the club’s political structure and call for judicial intervention.

The demonstration was the third organized supporter action in four days. Six supporter groups described it in advance as peaceful, democratic and organized. Reports from the site said the protest remained peaceful.

What the supporters want

Banners called for intervention and a “clean-up” of Corinthians. The demonstrators’ allegations concern the club’s administration, debts and internal politics; those allegations are demands for investigation, not findings established by a court.

The Public Prosecutor’s Office in São Paulo is examining a representation concerning the club. Whether that process could lead to any judicial intervention remains unresolved.

Current status: supporters have demanded intervention, but no court has ordered one. The club’s elected administration remains in place.

Memphis urges calm after video controversy

The protest followed controversy over a music video released by Memphis Depay that depicts Parque São Jorge in flames and criticizes a club vice-president. Memphis said the work had been made months earlier, was artistic rather than a call to action, and that he opposed violence and wanted supporters to express themselves peacefully.

Why it matters

Corinthians has reached the Libertadores quarter-finals, but the result has not quieted its institutional crisis. Sporting momentum, supporter pressure and official scrutiny are now running on parallel tracks.
