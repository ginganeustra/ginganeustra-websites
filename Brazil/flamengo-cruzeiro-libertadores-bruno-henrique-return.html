Bruno Henrique returns to training before Flamengo’s all-Brazil Libertadores decider

Bruno Henrique trained ahead of Flamengo vs. Cruzeiro on August 19, with the Copa Libertadores tie level at 1–1.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By BraGinga News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET

Bruno Henrique returned to group training on Monday and could be available when Flamengo hosts Cruzeiro in Wednesday’s Copa Libertadores round-of-16 second leg. The tie is level at 1–1 after the match in Belo Horizonte.

Ge reported that the forward missed the 5–1 win at Mirassol because of discomfort in his left ankle. His final availability is expected to depend on Tuesday’s training session, so his participation is possible rather than confirmed.

Everton Cebolinha also resumed field work after illness. Alex Sandro, Vitão and Nicolás de la Cruz remained in treatment, according to the same report. Coach Leonardo Jardim may restore several usual starters after rotating against Mirassol.

Kickoff at the Maracanã is Wednesday at 9:30 p.m. Brasília time, or 8:30 p.m. Eastern. A winner advances; another draw sends the all-Brazilian series to penalties.
