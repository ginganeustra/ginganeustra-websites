Flamengo crushes Mirassol 5–1 and closes to three points behind Palmeiras

Flamengo beat Mirassol 5–1 to reach 45 points, three behind Palmeiras with a match in hand in Brazil’s 2026 Série A title race.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By BraGinga News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET

Flamengo made the most of Palmeiras’ defeat, beating Mirassol 5–1 on Sunday to cut the Brasileirão lead to three points. Palmeiras has 48 from 23 matches; Flamengo has 45 from 22, meaning the challenger also has a game in hand.

Pedro scored twice as Leonardo Jardim’s squad delivered a reminder of its attacking depth even while managing players for the upcoming Copa Libertadores return against Cruzeiro. The margin also improved Flamengo’s goal difference to plus-25, four better than Palmeiras.

The strategic consequence is immediate. If Flamengo wins its outstanding match, it can draw level on points and potentially move ahead on tiebreakers. Ge noted that the three-point gap is the smallest between the leading contenders since March.

For Mirassol, the evening had the opposite effect. The club sits 17th on 23 points and must now travel to the altitude of Quito for a decisive Libertadores second leg against LDU after a 1–1 first-leg draw.
