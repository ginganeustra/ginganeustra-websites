Flamengo’s home gate crosses one million.

Sunday, 30 August 2026

Published 30 August 2026, 18:27 ET / 19:27 BRT

Flamengo have passed one million paying supporters at home matches in 2026, another measure of the club’s commercial weight as the title race tightens.

According to ge, 61,934 paying supporters attended Sunday’s 3–0 win over Botafogo. That took Flamengo’s season total to 1,044,968 paying spectators across 21 home-designated matches.

Twenty of those matches were played at the Maracanã and one at Raulino de Oliveira in Volta Redonda. The neutral-site Supercopa do Brasil was not included in the count.

Ge lists Flamengo’s average paying crowd for 2026 at 49,760 and its Brasileirão average at 58,203. The figures add context to the club’s revenue power as it continues to compete in multiple competitions and in the transfer market.

Source: ge, Flamengo surpasses one million home tickets sold in 2026.
