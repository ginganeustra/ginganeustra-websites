João Pedro signs with Chelsea through 2034.

The Brazil forward’s 20-goal season and club Player of the Season award have been followed by a new long-term contract.

BraGinga news desk · Published 22 August 2026, 11:00 ET / 12:00 BRT

João Pedro has signed a new Chelsea contract that runs through 2034, the English club announced Saturday.

The 24-year-old Brazil forward joined Chelsea from Brighton in July 2025. His first full season produced 20 goals in all competitions and Chelsea’s men’s Player of the Season award, as voted by supporters.

Why Chelsea moved now

Chelsea’s announcement presents the agreement as recognition of both output and growing responsibility within the squad. The club said João Pedro’s development as a leader had been perceptible during the season.

His defining early contribution came before the domestic campaign began. João Pedro scored against former club Fluminense in the 2025 Club World Cup semifinal and again in the final against Paris Saint-Germain.

Confirmed: Chelsea says João Pedro’s new contract runs until 2034. The announcement does not describe an option beyond that date.

The Brazil angle

A contract of this length gives one of Brazil’s leading forwards uncommon long-term stability at club level. Selection remains Carlo Ancelotti’s decision, but João Pedro enters the next international cycle with his Chelsea status reinforced.
