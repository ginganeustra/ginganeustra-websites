Flamengo beat Mirassol to move within one point.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 23:37 ET / 3 September, 00:37 BRT

Flamengo beat Mirassol 2–0 at the Maracanã on Wednesday, 2 September, turning their game in hand into three points and reducing Palmeiras’ Brasileirão lead to one point.

Jorge Carrascal opened the scoring and Lucas Paquetá added the second before halftime. Flamengo’s official report confirms the final score in the rescheduled fourth-round fixture.

The result leaves Flamengo on 51 points and Palmeiras on 52, with both clubs now having played 25 matches. The title-race comparison no longer needs an adjustment for Flamengo’s outstanding game.

UOL’s match report describes a controlled second half in which Flamengo protected the advantage rather than allowing Mirassol a route back into the contest.

Mirassol remain on 25 points after 25 matches. The complete Brasileirão table is embedded in the edition, including the updated matches-played totals.
