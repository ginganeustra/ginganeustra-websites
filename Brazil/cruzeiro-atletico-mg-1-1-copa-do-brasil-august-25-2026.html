Cruzeiro 1–1 Atlético-MG: Minas stays dead even.

Two second-half strikes — Keny Arroyo for Cruzeiro and Renan Lodi for Atlético-MG — left the quarter-final level heading to the Arena MRV.

25 August 2026 · final

The first Copa do Brasil quarter-final of the week finished without separation. Cruzeiro and Atlético-MG drew 1–1 at the Mineirão in front of more than 59,000 spectators, setting up a winner-take-all second leg on September 1.

The first half was cautious and broken up by stoppages. The match opened after the interval when Arroyo struck from outside the area to put Cruzeiro ahead. Atlético answered ten minutes later through Renan Lodi, finishing a move created from the left after Cuello’s involvement.

There was also a serious-looking first-half incident involving Atlético defender Ruan, who hit his head on the turf and left under the concussion protocol. Club officials later said he was doing well and had been taken to hospital for checks.

What the draw means

There is no aggregate advantage. A win for either side at the Arena MRV sends that club to the semi-finals; another draw sends the tie to penalties. The return leg is scheduled for Tuesday, September 1 at 21:00 BRT.

A record crowd for this Copa do Brasil

Ge reported an attendance of about 59,200, the biggest crowd of the 2026 Copa do Brasil to this point, with gate receipts of roughly R$4.9 million.

Next

Before the return leg, Cruzeiro goes to Vasco in the Brasileirão on Saturday, while Atlético-MG hosts Vitória.
