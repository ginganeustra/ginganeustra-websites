Brasileirão · Round 24 final · 24 August 2026

Viveros puts Athletico one point behind Flamengo.

Athletico-PR beat Botafogo 3–2 at the Nilton Santos after building a three-goal lead and surviving a late response.

Published 24 August 2026 · 23:00 ET / 25 August · 00:00 BRT

João Cruz opened the scoring after 10 minutes and Kevin Viveros doubled the advantage two minutes later. Botafogo created chances, but Athletico goalkeeper Santos and the post kept the home side out.

Viveros makes the decisive break

Viveros scored again in the 78th minute after Kerwin Vargas carried the counterattack and found him in space. The Colombian striker’s second goal took him to 16 in the Brasileirão, one ahead of Flamengo’s Pedro.

Botafogo’s late reply

Santi Rodríguez scored in the 84th and 91st minutes, cutting a 3–0 deficit to one. Athletico held on, however, to reach 44 points—one behind second-place Flamengo. Botafogo remains 11th on 30 points.

Athletico has now completed 24 matches; Botafogo has played 23. The complete BraGinga table reflects Monday’s final result.
