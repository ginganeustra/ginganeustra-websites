CBF releases the review behind Wallisson’s red card.

The federation has published the VAR analysis from Santos’s 1–1 draw with Mirassol, adding an official record to a decision the visitors continue to dispute.

Published 24 August 2026 · 11:13 ET / 12:13 BRT

Wallisson had initially been shown a yellow card for his challenge on Neymar during Sunday’s completed Brasileirão match. After a video review at the pitch-side monitor, referee Edina Alves Batista upgraded the punishment to a red card. Santos and Mirassol ultimately drew 1–1.

What the CBF has added

The CBF’s official analysis page publishes the video-assistant review from the Round 24 match. The release documents the intervention and the referee’s subsequent change of decision; it does not erase the disagreement over whether the incident met the threshold for a red card.

Why the argument continues

Mirassol coach Rafael Guanaes criticized the officiating after the match. Former referees Carlos Eugênio Simon and Paulo César Oliveira also argued publicly that the video assistant should not have intervened. Their assessments are commentary, not an official reversal of the match decision.

The result and the standings remain unchanged. This report concerns the official post-match review only; BraGinga does not report matches in progress.
