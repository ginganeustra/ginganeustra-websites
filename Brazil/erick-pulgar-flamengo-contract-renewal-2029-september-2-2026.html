Erick Pulgar renews Flamengo contract through 2029.

Thursday, 3 September 2026

Published 3 September 2026, 11:37 ET / 12:37 BRT

Erick Pulgar has renewed his Flamengo contract through 2029. The club announced the signed agreement on Wednesday, 2 September, with the Chilean midfielder alongside football director José Boto.

Pulgar, 32, joined Flamengo in August 2022. At the time of the announcement, the club credited him with 172 appearances, six goals and nine assists. Those figures preceded Wednesday night’s match against Mirassol.

Flamengo’s announcement lists Pulgar among the players involved in their 2025 Brasileirão and Copa Libertadores title campaigns. In comments published by the club, he said remaining at Flamengo was what he and his family wanted.

UOL reported that the parties had already reached an understanding before completing the signing. The new agreement runs to the end of 2029.

The renewal was announced on the same day that Flamengo confirmed Wallace Yan’s permanent transfer to Red Bull Bragantino.

Sources: Flamengo’s renewal announcement; UOL’s confirmation.
