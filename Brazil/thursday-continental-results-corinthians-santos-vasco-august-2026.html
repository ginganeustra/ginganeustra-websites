Continental knockouts · Updated 20 August 2026, 23:15 ET

Corinthians through; Santos and Vasco advance

Corinthians beat Rosario Central 1–0 at the Neo Química Arena after a scoreless first leg. Breno Bidon scored from a Memphis Depay assist after Raniele’s second-half dismissal. Corinthians will meet Estudiantes in the Libertadores quarter-finals.

Mirassol’s first Libertadores knockout run ended in Quito. The return against LDU finished 0–0, following a 1–1 first leg, before LDU won the shootout 5–4. Palmeiras will face LDU next.

In the Sudamericana, Santos drew 0–0 at Macará to protect its 2–1 first-leg lead. Vasco won 4–1 at Olimpia after a goalless opener. Botafogo beat Cienciano 1–0 at Nilton Santos but was eliminated 6–2 on aggregate after the first-leg collapse in Cusco.

Sources: UOL on Corinthians–Rosario Central; UOL on LDU–Mirassol; UOL on Macará–Santos; UOL on Botafogo–Cienciano.
