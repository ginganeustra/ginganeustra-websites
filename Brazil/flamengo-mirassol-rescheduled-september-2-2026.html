Flamengo’s game in hand now has a date.

The CBF has scheduled Flamengo–Mirassol for Sept. 2, giving the title race a defined point at which the unequal-games column disappears.

The fourth-round match will be played at the Maracanã at 7:30 p.m. BRT on Wednesday, Sept. 2, according to the CBF schedule reported by UOL. The date became available after both clubs were eliminated from the Copa do Brasil.

That will end Flamengo’s game-in-hand advantage over Palmeiras and the rest of the leading group. The significance now is simple: every result in the league race can be read against an exact date for the catch-up match, rather than an open fixture.

Source: UOL — CBF date for Flamengo–Mirassol.
