Vasco climb out with a statement win.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 23:01 ET / 00:01 BRT

Vasco beat Cruzeiro 3–1 at São Januário and ended Saturday outside the Brasileirão relegation zone.

Tchê Tchê opened the scoring in the 33rd minute before Lucas Freitas doubled the advantage 10 minutes into the second half. David made it 3–0 in added time. Felipe Morais answered for Cruzeiro moments later, but the result was secure.

The win took Vasco to 25 points from 24 matches, ahead of Internacional on wins. Cruzeiro remain fifth on 39 points after fielding a much-changed side before Tuesday’s Copa do Brasil return against Atlético-MG.

Two more 2–1 home wins

Atlético-MG used a reserve-heavy lineup and defeated Vitória 2–1. Thiago Borbas and Reinier put the hosts two goals ahead before Erick scored in the 87th minute. Atlético reached 36 points and moved seventh.

São Paulo ended a 10-match, four-month Brasileirão winless run by beating Red Bull Bragantino 2–1. Luciano converted a penalty and 20-year-old Gustavo Santana scored his first senior goal. Fernando’s late reply did not prevent São Paulo from climbing to 11th.

Saturday’s three matches are final. Six Round 25 games are scheduled Sunday, so BraGinga will update the complete table again only after those results are confirmed.
