Fluminense goes to Mendoza level with Independiente Rivadavia after a 0–0 first leg

Independiente Rivadavia hosts Fluminense on August 18 at 6 p.m. ET, with their Copa Libertadores round-of-16 tie level at 0–0.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By BraGinga News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET

Fluminense and Independiente Rivadavia begin Tuesday’s return in Mendoza with everything unresolved after a 0–0 first leg at the Maracanã. Kickoff at Estadio Malvinas Argentinas is 7 p.m. local time, or 6 p.m. Eastern.

Interim coach Marcão is expected to restore Thiago Silva and Ignácio in central defence after both missed the weekend win over Palmeiras for different reasons. Ge reported that Riquelme Felipe, Jefferson Savarino and Igor Rabello are unavailable or dealing with injury concerns.

The contrast in form is striking. Fluminense arrives after the emotional 3–2 comeback against Palmeiras, while Independiente Rivadavia lost 2–0 to Belgrano in the Clausura. Neither domestic result changes the clean 0–0 aggregate.

Any winner advances. A draw after 90 minutes would take the tie to penalties. For Fluminense, a return to the quarter-finals would also be the first major verdict on the club’s decision to move on from Zubeldía.
