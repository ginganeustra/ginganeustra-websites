Palmeiras held; Flamengo move within four.

Sunday, 30 August 2026

Published 30 August 2026, 18:27 ET · Updated 23:14 ET / 31 August, 00:14 BRT

Flamengo cut Palmeiras’ lead to three points by beating Botafogo 3–0 at the Maracanã, while Santos ended their winless run in São Paulo derbies with a 1–0 victory at Corinthians.

Samuel Lino scored twice for Flamengo and Jorge Carrascal added the third. The win moved Flamengo to 48 points from 24 matches, three behind Palmeiras before the leaders’ later Sunday fixture.

At the Neo Química Arena, Christian Oliva scored the only goal as Santos beat Corinthians 1–0. The result lifted Santos to 29 points from 24 matches. Corinthians remain on 32.

Six goals in Curitiba

Athletico-PR and Fluminense drew 3–3 in the day’s early match. Leozinho, Benavídez and Viveros scored for Athletico; Hulk, Canobbio and Ignácio replied for Fluminense. Viveros converted a late penalty to rescue the draw.

Athletico moved to 45 points and Fluminense to 42.

Palmeiras held in Mirassol

Mirassol drew 1–1 with Palmeiras after Eduardo opened the scoring early in the second half and Mauricio equalized in the 74th minute. Arthur was sent off for Palmeiras in added time.

Palmeiras lead the table with 52 points from 25 matches. Flamengo are four points behind with a match in hand. Mirassol’s point moves them out of the relegation places on the competition tiebreakers.

Bahia fifth; Grêmio pull clear

Bahia beat Internacional 3–2 at Fonte Nova to reach 40 points and climb to fifth. The defeat leaves Internacional in the relegation zone on 25 points.

Grêmio defeated Chapecoense 3–1 in Porto Alegre, moving to 28 points and 15th. Chapecoense remain bottom with 14.
