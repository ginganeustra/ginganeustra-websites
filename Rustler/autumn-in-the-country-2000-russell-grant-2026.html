Autumn in the Country gets $2,000 from Township

Council acted before the festival after changing budget figures raised questions for staff.

By The Rustler staff · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated 4:25 p.m.

EMBRUN — Russell council has approved a $2,000 grant for this month’s Autumn in the Country festival after councillors decided organizers needed financial certainty before the event.

The request produced an unusually long discussion because the festival’s budget had changed substantially during the year. Staff told council the figures had moved from an original budget of about $20,000 to roughly $6,200 and later back to approximately $12,000 as grant funding and event plans changed.

That prompted staff to seek more information before bringing forward a recommendation.

Coun. Lisa Deacon argued that waiting until the next council meeting would leave organizers too little time before the Sept. 18-20 festival.

She used a simple example: an organizer who needed $2,000 for hot dogs would benefit far more from knowing the money was available two weeks before an event than three days before it.

Council ultimately agreed and approved the $2,000.

Autumn in the Country will also host the Township’s 2026 Photo Expo, where images submitted by local photographers will be displayed. This year’s theme is Cultivation, and four selected photographs are to become outdoor public art along the New York Central Recreational Trail.
