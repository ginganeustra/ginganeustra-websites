$140 fire charge replaces old $100 fee

Council approved a $40 increase to an existing fire-service charge as part of an actual-cost-recovery approach.

By The Rustler staff · Originally reported September 3, 2026 · Restored September 6

RUSSELL TOWNSHIP — One Russell Township fire-service charge is going from $100 to $140 after council approved a fee change intended to better reflect the municipality’s actual cost.

The item came before council at its Aug. 31 regular meeting. The change adds $40 to the existing $100 charge.

Council’s discussion framed the increase as cost recovery rather than a new source of general revenue: the objective was to bring the charge closer to what the service costs the Township to provide.

The surviving TWP draft and meeting transcript preserve the $100-to-$140 decision, but the exact equipment or service label is not clear enough in the recovered record to restate safely. This restored version therefore does not guess at that label.

The increase should not be read as a blanket $140 charge on every fire-service activity. It applies to the specific fee council changed in the Aug. 31 item.
