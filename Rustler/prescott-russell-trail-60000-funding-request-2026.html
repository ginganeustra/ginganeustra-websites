Trail group seeks another $60,000 from Prescott-Russell

The counties want more information before deciding on a new funding arrangement for the 72-kilometre recreational trail.

By The Rustler staff · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated 4:25 p.m.

The organization operating the Prescott and Russell Recreational Trail is asking the United Counties for an additional $60,000 as the two sides prepare to negotiate a new agreement.

The 72-kilometre multi-use trail runs from the Ottawa boundary east toward St-Eugène and is an important recreational link through the region. Management was transferred by the United Counties of Prescott and Russell to an arm’s-length corporation in 2022.

Trail executive director Eric Collard appeared before county council in late August asking for public funding equivalent to roughly one dollar for every UCPR resident.

The organization says it has also worked to diversify its finances through corporate sponsorship.

County council did not simply approve the additional money. Russell Mayor Mike Tarnowski was among those discussing whether the funding question should instead be considered as part of the 2027 budget, allowing the counties to look at the trail’s needs as part of the broader financial picture.

The request comes at an uncertain time for recreational trails across Eastern Ontario because of the proposed Alto high-speed rail project and questions about future transportation corridors.

For Russell-area residents, however, the trail is not an abstract regional asset. It connects directly into the local trail network used by cyclists, walkers and runners between Russell and Embrun.
