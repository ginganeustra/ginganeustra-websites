Embrun water tower delayed again

The tower is now expected back in service near the end of September. Restrictions remain while more extensive work is completed.

By The Rustler staff · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated 4:25 p.m.

EMBRUN — Residents waiting for normal water service will have to wait longer.

Russell Township administration told council Aug. 31 that the Embrun water tower is now expected to return to service near the end of September, about a month later than the most recent target.

Staff said the remaining work proved more extensive and took longer than first anticipated. Water restrictions therefore have to remain in place until the tower is back online and it is safe to lift them.

The tower has been out of service since October 2025 after problems were identified that required it to remain empty during repairs. Restrictions were imposed to preserve enough water in the system for household needs and firefighting.

The rules were later relaxed to allow limited outdoor water use, but normal service has not yet returned.

The latest delay prompted Coun. Charles Armstrong to raise another question: should affected Embrun residents eventually receive some form of rebate?

Armstrong argued that residents have paid the fixed portion of their water charges while living with reduced service for an extended period.

Administration noted that water bills include both consumption and fixed charges. Residents who use less water already pay less on the consumption portion, while reducing the fixed fee would take money away from reserves used to replace water infrastructure.

There is another complication: Russell’s water tower will itself require work in the future.

Mayor Mike Tarnowski suggested it might make more sense to look at the question once work on both towers is known, rather than creating one rebate arrangement for Embrun and another later for Russell.

No rebate was approved. For now, the immediate news is simpler: the tower is not ready, restrictions continue, and the new target is the end of September.
