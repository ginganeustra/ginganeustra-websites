Province puts $20,425 into Embrun Main Street improvements

The municipal parking area at Notre-Dame and Blais is in line for landscaping, lighting, bike facilities, signs and active-transportation markings.

By The Rustler staff · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated 4:25 p.m.

EMBRUN — A small but visible section of downtown Embrun is getting a provincial makeover.

Glengarry—Prescott—Russell MPP Stéphane Sarrazin announced $20,425 in provincial funding during an Aug. 28 visit to Russell Township. The money will go toward improvements around the municipal parking lot at Notre-Dame and Blais streets, beside the New York Central Recreational Trail.

The project is being funded through Ontario’s Rural Ontario Development Program Community Development Stream.

Plans include landscaping, decorative lighting, parklet furniture, a bicycle rack and repair station, wayfinding and safety signs and new markings for active transportation.

The parking lot sits at an important entry point to the recreational trail linking Embrun and Russell.

Most of the physical improvements are expected to be carried out in spring 2027.

The announcement comes while Notre-Dame itself is undergoing major reconstruction and while the Township is looking at longer-term changes intended to make the village core more pedestrian- and business-friendly.
