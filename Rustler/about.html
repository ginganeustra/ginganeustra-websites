The Rustler

Independent local news for Russell Township.

The Rustler covers Russell, Embrun, Marionville, Limoges and the issues that affect people across Russell Township.

Our reporting priorities include municipal government, roads and construction, policing and emergency services, public health, schools, agriculture, local business, community organizations, sports, utilities and the United Counties of Prescott and Russell.

We aim to write local news in plain, direct language while preserving the details readers need to understand decisions that affect their taxes, services, streets and community.

Corrections and updates are made when new information becomes available.
