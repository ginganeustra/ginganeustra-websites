Recreation complex still aiming for November occupancy

The latest contractor schedule still points to Nov. 5. January ice planning is beginning, but staff will not promise dates until construction is more certain.

By The Rustler staff · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated 4:25 p.m.

EMBRUN — The latest contractor schedule for Russell Township’s new recreation complex still points to Nov. 5 for occupancy, council was told Aug. 31.

That date comes from the contractor’s July 31 progress report. Staff expected to receive the August report shortly and planned to bring council a fuller construction and operational update during September.

The timing is particularly important for hockey and other ice users who want to know when the complex’s three pads will actually be available.

Regular ice users have been told they are expected to retain the same hours for the January-to-April period.

For the third ice pad, recreation staff is building a provisional schedule using requests received from prospective new users before June 30. Those groups will be contacted once the Township has enough confidence in the opening date to make commitments.

If time remains after new requests are handled, additional hours could then be offered to existing users.

The Township is deliberately avoiding promising ice before it knows the building can deliver it.

That caution matters on a project of this size. Russell is simultaneously preparing to borrow approximately $70 million for the facility, making the construction schedule and operating date two of the municipality’s biggest issues heading into the fall.
