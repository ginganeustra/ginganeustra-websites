Recreation complex still aiming for November occupancy

The latest contractor schedule still points to Nov. 5. January ice planning is beginning, but staff will not promise dates until construction is more certain.

By The Rustler staff · September 5, 2026 · Updated 7:00 p.m.

EMBRUN — The newest timetable available to Russell Township still points to Nov. 5 for occupancy of the new recreation complex.

The date comes from the general contractor’s July 31 report. Staff told council Aug. 31 that a fresh August report was expected shortly, with a fuller operational and construction update to follow in September.

“It was still on November 5th. That’s for the occupancy,” recreation staff told councillors when asked whether the opening target had moved.

The date matters most immediately to hockey and other ice users trying to plan the winter. Regular users have been told they are expected to keep the same hours for the January-to-April period.

The third ice pad creates a second scheduling job. Staff is assembling a mock schedule from requests submitted by prospective new users before June 30, then plans to contact those groups as the opening becomes more certain.

Officials do not want to sell ice time before they know the building can deliver it. Staff said the Township wants to “make sure so that we don’t disappoint anybody.”

“Right now, we’re working on a mock-up schedule for the third ice pad.” — Russell Township recreation staff

If space remains after the new requests are worked into the third-pad schedule, additional time could then be offered to regular users.

Coun. Lisa Deacon raised the issue because, as she put it, residents are watching construction “in much anticipation,” especially people waiting to learn when they can get onto the three new ice pads.

The November date is an occupancy target, not a blanket promise that every part of the facility will immediately be open for every use. The next contractor report will be the first major test of whether the fall schedule is still holding.
