Local election

Tarnowski acclaimed; five candidates chase four council seats

There will be no mayoral contest this fall, but Russell voters will still choose four councillors from a field of five.

By The Rustler staff · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated 4:25 p.m.

RUSSELL TOWNSHIP — Mike Tarnowski will remain mayor of Russell Township for another four years without having to fight an election campaign.

Tarnowski was acclaimed after the Aug. 21 nomination deadline passed with no challenger for the mayor’s office.

There will, however, be a race for council.

Five candidates are registered for the Township’s four councillor positions: incumbents Charles Armstrong, Lisa Deacon and Jamie Laurin, along with Dominique Bertrand and Nick Gallagher. Current councillor Marc Lalonde is not on the candidate list for another council term.

The voting period begins Oct. 21 at 9 a.m. and runs through Oct. 26 at 8 p.m. Residents will be able to vote using the methods set out by the Township’s election office.

The lack of a mayoral race does not make October’s vote meaningless. Four of the five council candidates will help decide budgets, taxation, recreation-complex financing, growth, roads, water infrastructure and the next stages of Notre-Dame reconstruction during a period when Russell Township continues to grow rapidly.
