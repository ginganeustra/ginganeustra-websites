Clausura · Round 7 finals

Instituto reclaim Zone A lead as Round 7 closes.

Monday, 31 August 2026

Published 31 August 2026, 23:33 ET / 1 September, 00:33 ART

Instituto beat San Lorenzo 1–0 in Córdoba to finish Round 7 at the top of Zone A. Giuliano Cerato scored the match’s only goal in the 53rd minute.

Instituto reached 16 points from seven matches, one ahead of Gimnasia Mendoza. San Lorenzo remain on seven points and drop to 14th.

Defensa move to third

Héctor David Martínez scored in the 75th minute as Defensa y Justicia defeated Platense 1–0. Defensa move to 14 points, behind Gimnasia Mendoza and two points off Instituto.

Two scoreless draws

Estudiantes and Newell’s finished 0–0 in La Plata. Newell’s move to 11 points and fifth in Zone A; Estudiantes have seven points.

Tigre and Barracas Central also drew 0–0. Tigre climbed to second in Zone B with 12 points on goal difference, while Barracas reached 10 points.

Sources: TyC Sports Round 7 results; ESPN, Defensa 1–0 Platense; ESPN, Estudiantes 0–0 Newell’s; TyC Sports, Tigre 0–0 Barracas; TyC Sports, Instituto 1–0 San Lorenzo.
