Sarmiento win 2–0 as Round 8 gets underway

Saturday, 5 September 2026

Published 5 September 2026, 08:24 ET / 09:24 ART

Original La Nuestra editorial illustration. No third-party photo assets or club marks.

Sarmiento opened Clausura Round 8 with a 2–0 away win over Estudiantes de Río Cuarto on Friday, moving the Junín club to 15 points in Zone B.

Gonzalo Maffini’s own goal put Sarmiento ahead early. Junior Marabel added the second from the penalty spot in the second half.

The result leaves Sarmiento one point behind Zone B leader Argentinos Juniors, although Argentinos still have their Round 8 match to play. Belgrano’s 1–1 draw with Huracán moved Belgrano to 12 points and Huracán to 10.

In Zone A, Platense and Deportivo Riestra also drew 1–1. Both clubs moved to nine points after eight matches.
