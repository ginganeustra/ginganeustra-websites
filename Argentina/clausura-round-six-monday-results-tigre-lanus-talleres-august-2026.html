Torneo Clausura · Round 6 final · 24 August 2026

Di María rescues Rosario; Tigre climbs.

Monday’s three completed matches leave Argentinos Juniors atop Zone B and bring Round 6 to a close.

Published 24 August 2026 · 23:00 ET / 25 August · 00:00 ART

Tigre 2–1 Central Córdoba

Tigre took the only victory of the night and climbed to 11 points in Zone B. Central Córdoba remains on six points in Zone A after its fourth loss in six matches.

Lanús 1–1 Argentinos Juniors

Felipe Peña Biafore put Lanús ahead before Gonzalo Verón equalized. Argentinos moved to 13 points and finished the round alone atop Zone B; Lanús reached seven points but remains outside Zone A’s top eight.

Talleres 2–2 Rosario Central

Agustín Álvarez Martínez converted a penalty and Valentín Depietri doubled Talleres’ lead. Ángel Di María then scored twice from the penalty spot to earn Rosario Central a point. Rosario finishes the round fourth in Zone B on 11 points; Talleres remains last in Zone A with four.
