Montenegro leaves San Lorenzo for Al-Wahda.

San Lorenzo has disclosed the compensation, retained rights and broader financial settlement behind the 21-year-old centre-back’s departure.

Published 24 August 2026 · 11:13 ET / 12:13 ART

San Lorenzo confirmed on Sunday that it had agreed to terminate Lautaro Montenegro’s contract so the academy graduate can continue his career with Al-Wahda in the United Arab Emirates.

The disclosed terms

Al-Wahda will pay San Lorenzo US$200,000 in compensation. The Buenos Aires club retains 20% of the defender’s economic rights, preserving a stake in a possible future transfer.

A wider settlement

San Lorenzo says Montenegro’s representative, Patricio Lombilla, also waived approximately US$400,000 in claims connected with previous operations involving Agustín Martegani, Santiago Sosa, Tomás Silva and Nahuel Barrios. The agreement includes a release intended to prevent future claims over those matters.

Montenegro’s San Lorenzo contract was due to expire on Dec. 31. The club says he rejected an improved renewal offer, left pre-season preparations and signed a pre-contract with Al-Wahda. Al-Wahda has since presented him as a new player.

Primary source: San Lorenzo’s official agreement notice, published Aug. 23, 2026. The official notice is the basis for all financial figures in this report.
