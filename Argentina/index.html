UPDATED · AFA legal response

Argentina moves from punishment to appeal

AFA legal manager Andrés Paton Urich says the association was notified of six disciplinary decisions and will appeal. He described the sanctions on Leandro Paredes, Nahuel Molina and Roberto Ayala as excessive and said the AFA will seek to have them reduced.

The legal response does not cancel the issued penalties: Paredes has 10 matches, Molina seven, Thiago Almada one and Ayala three, while the AFA was fined US$321,000. Any change now depends on the appeal process.

Libertadores · Estudiantes

Joaquín Correa and Guido Carrillo send Estudiantes through in Chile

Estudiantes beat Universidad Católica 3–0. Correa scored before halftime and Carrillo added two after it. With the first leg 1–1, Estudiantes advances 4–1 on aggregate.

Platense holds Coquimbo scoreless and wins the shootout

Mendoza’s run ends with Fábio decisive from the spot
