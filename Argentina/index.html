River 2–2 Vélez

River lets a two-goal night slip away

River controlled the first half and went 2–0 up, but Vélez recovered after the break and earned a 2–2 draw. The result keeps Vélez unbeaten and leaves River with only four points from six Clausura matches.

TyC Sports — Aug. 23

Racing 1–1 Boca

Rojo scores on Boca; Merentiel answers

Marcos Rojo converted an early penalty against his former club before Miguel Merentiel levelled for Boca. Neither side found a winner, leaving Boca on seven points in Zone A and Racing on five in Zone B.

Sarmiento 2–0 Estudiantes

Sarmiento's run is now a real table story

Mauricio Martínez and Gastón González scored as Sarmiento won a fourth straight Clausura match. The Junín club reached 12 points, level with Argentinos Juniors before Monday's remaining fixtures.

Platense turns it around; Defensa wins in Córdoba
