What Argentina is talking about

Clausura · Round 7 finals

River answer immediately under Ponzio; Argentinos turn it around

River won 3–2 at Banfield in Leonardo Ponzio’s first match as interim coach. Argentinos Juniors came from behind to beat Aldosivi 2–1 and move to 16 points in Zone B.

River Plate · Manager watch

Ponzio’s first match produces three goals and three points

Sebastián Driussi, Thiago Almada and Ángel Correa scored before Banfield made the finish tense. Ponzio says his appointment remains interim.

River Plate · Market watch

Joaquín Freitas is reported close to Flamengo

TyC Sports reports a proposed US$17-million deal including bonuses. Neither club has announced the transfer.

Argentinos Juniors · Zone B

Lescano and Verón flip the match after halftime

Argentinos recovered from Andrés Vombergar’s opener to beat Aldosivi 2–1 and reach 16 points from seven matches.

Argentina abroad · Market watch

Atlético board rules out Julián Álvarez sale to Barcelona

Atlético’s directors say there will be no negotiation with Barcelona. The statement closes that destination, not every possible future transfer.

Argentines abroad · Manager watch

Kily González takes Inter Miami job, pending work permits

Guillermo Hoyos remains in interim control until the former Argentina international receives authorization and arrives with his staff.

Clausura · Saturday finals

Miramón gives Gimnasia a late win in Rosario

Gimnasia won 2–1 after Jaminton Campaz had levelled in the 86th minute. Two late Saturday matches finished goalless.

Clausura · Zone A leaders

Vélez goes top after fighting back at Riestra

Tomás González headed Riestra in front before a Facundo Miño own goal earned Vélez the point.

Clausura · Friday results

Belmonte settles Boca–Lanús; Unión score four

Boca moved to 10 points in Zone A with a 90+3 winner; Unión’s 4–1 interzonal win left Sarmiento on 12.
