Sunday match day

Round 6 · interzonal

Five Sunday fixtures, two major night games

Sarmiento–Estudiantes LP and Barracas Central–Platense begin at 14:45 ART. Belgrano–Defensa y Justicia follows at 17:00. River hosts Vélez at 19:15 before Racing–Boca closes the night at 21:30.

River comes off its first Clausura win, 2–0 over Argentinos Juniors. Vélez is unbeaten. Racing and Boca each enter the clásico with only one league victory and need points to move toward the top-eight cutoff.
