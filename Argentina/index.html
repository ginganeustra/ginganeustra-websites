Lead · Rosario Central

Central gets the extra man, but Corinthians gets the goal

Raniele’s 57th-minute dismissal gave Rosario Central more than half an hour with a numerical advantage. Central created pressure but could not beat Hugo Souza. Memphis Depay then crossed and Breno Bidon scored in the 79th minute.

The 1–0 result settled the aggregate after a scoreless first leg. Central exits; Corinthians advances to meet Estudiantes.
