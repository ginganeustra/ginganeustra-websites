The late-window question is no longer “who is available?” but “who can actually sign?”

Argentina’s winter window is closed. Liga Profesional clubs can add only if an overseas sale creates the route. That makes outgoing business, not transfer gossip, the key to every remaining squad move. Chimy Ávila is official at Independiente; Boca have loaned Marcelo Weigandt to Liga de Quito.

Lead · Boca Juniors

Boca turns a comfortable advantage into a rout and earns São Paulo

Boca Juniors beat Deportivo Recoleta 4–0 in Asunción, with goals from Santiago Ascacíbar, Sebastián Villa, Miguel Merentiel and Alan Velasco. Added to the 3–1 first leg, that makes the aggregate 7–1. Enner Valencia also made his Boca debut.

The reward is a Sudamericana quarter-final against São Paulo, which eliminated Bolívar. It gives La Nuestra a major Argentina–Brazil tie to follow in September.

Libertadores · Estudiantes

Joaquín Correa and Guido Carrillo send Estudiantes through in Chile

Estudiantes beat Universidad Católica 3–0. Correa scored before halftime and Carrillo added two after it. With the first leg 1–1, Estudiantes advances 4–1 on aggregate.

Libertadores · Platense

Platense holds Coquimbo scoreless and wins the shootout

After a 1–1 first leg, the return in Chile finished 0–0. Platense won the penalty shootout 7–6 and will meet Fluminense in the quarter-finals.

Libertadores · Independiente Rivadavia

Mendoza’s run ends with Fábio decisive from the spot

Independiente Rivadavia drew 1–1 with Fluminense after Álex Arce’s stoppage-time penalty, but lost the shootout 5–4. The result ends a notable Libertadores run for the Mendoza club.
