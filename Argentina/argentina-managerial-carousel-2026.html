Twenty clubs changed coach. That is a football story, not background noise.

Olé’s survey finds that only eight managers who started the year with a Liga Profesional club remain in the same job. The churn changes training, selection, recruitment and the meaning of every poor fortnight.

La Nuestra will distinguish dismissals, resignations, interim arrangements and confirmed appointments, then explain what changes for the squad rather than recycling candidate lists as fact.

Sources: Olé manager survey; TyC Sports tracker.
