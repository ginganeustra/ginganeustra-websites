River Plate · Sudamericana · manager change

River are out — and Coudet is gone.

Independiente Santa Fe eliminated River 8–7 on penalties after a 1–1 draw at the Monumental. Eduardo Coudet then left the coaching job.

Published 28 August 2026 · 01:10 ET / 02:10 ART

River Plate's continental season has collapsed at the round-of-16 stage of the Copa Sudamericana. After a 1–1 draw with Independiente Santa Fe at the Monumental, River lost the shootout 8–7.

A costly exit

The defeat removes one of River's main routes back to continental football in 2027 and leaves the Clausura and the annual table as the principal paths still available. River sit near the bottom of Zone B after six rounds, making the domestic recovery urgent.

Coudet leaves

River confirmed Thursday that Eduardo Coudet is no longer the club's coach. The decision followed the Santa Fe elimination, with Coudet describing the process as unsustainable in remarks reported after his departure.

The timing adds another layer to Sunday's league trip to Banfield. River now enter Round 7 needing points while also managing a coaching transition.

Pressure from the stands

Supporter anger spilled into the Monumental after the elimination, with chants aimed at the team and club leadership. The reaction underlined how quickly River's ambitious post-World Cup rebuild has turned into a crisis.
