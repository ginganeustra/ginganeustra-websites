Clausura · Round 6 · Saturday

Gimnasia Mendoza and Newell’s win Saturday’s Clausura thrillers

Gimnasia Mendoza won 3–2 at Gimnasia La Plata and Newell’s beat Banfield 2–1 as five Saturday finals reshaped both Clausura zones.

By Argentina La Nuestra News Desk · Published 23:00 ET / 00:00 ART
Gimnasia LP 2–3 Gimnasia Mendoza
Atlético Tucumán 0–0 Instituto
Independiente 0–0 Independiente Rivadavia
Newell’s 2–1 Banfield
Huracán 0–0 Deportivo Riestra

Gimnasia Mendoza won the day’s only five-goal contest in La Plata. The 3–2 victory lifts the Mendoza club to 12 points and second place in Zone A, one point behind Instituto.

Newell’s Old Boys also took all three points, defeating Banfield 2–1 in Rosario. Newell’s moves to fifth in Zone A on 10 points, while Banfield remains 11th in Zone B on seven.

Three scoreless draws

Instituto’s 0–0 draw at Atlético Tucumán was enough to preserve first place in Zone A. Independiente and Independiente Rivadavia also finished 0–0, as did Huracán and Deportivo Riestra.

Sunday’s centre stage

River Plate hosts Vélez and Racing welcomes Boca in Sunday’s headline interzonal fixtures. Sarmiento–Estudiantes, Barracas Central–Platense and Belgrano–Defensa y Justicia complete the day.

The qualification line

Instituto and Gimnasia Mendoza lead Zone A. In Zone B, Argentinos Juniors remains first, while Atlético Tucumán, Gimnasia La Plata and Huracán all added points on Saturday. The full 30-team classification is embedded on the La Nuestra front page.
