Continental roundup · 19 August 2026

Boca rolls, Estudiantes dominates and Platense survives

Three Argentine clubs move into continental quarter-finals in two nights; Independiente Rivadavia is eliminated by Fluminense on penalties.

← Back to La Nuestra

Boca’s 4–0 win sets up São Paulo

Boca Juniors took a 3–1 advantage to Asunción and never allowed Deportivo Recoleta back into the tie. Santiago Ascacíbar opened the scoring, Sebastián Villa added the second before halftime, Miguel Merentiel scored early in the second half and Alan Velasco completed a 4–0 win. The two legs add up to a 7–1 aggregate victory.

Enner Valencia made his Boca debut. Boca’s quarter-final opponent will be São Paulo, which beat Bolívar 3–1 after a 1–1 first leg.

Estudiantes wins 3–0 in Chile

Estudiantes de La Plata turned a 1–1 first leg into a decisive 4–1 aggregate victory over Universidad Católica. Joaquín Correa scored in the 44th minute, then Guido Carrillo struck in the 53rd and 83rd minutes. The 3–0 away victory puts Estudiantes into the Libertadores quarter-finals.

Platense survives a shootout in Coquimbo

The first leg in Argentina ended 1–1. The second leg in Chile finished 0–0, leaving the tie level and sending it to penalties. Platense won the shootout 7–6 and will face Fluminense in the last eight.

Independiente Rivadavia’s run ends

Independiente Rivadavia forced a shootout against Fluminense when Álex Arce converted a stoppage-time penalty for 1–1. Fluminense goalkeeper Fábio then saved two penalties as the Brazilian club won the shootout 5–4.

River holds the line; Tigre goes out

River Plate drew 0–0 at Independiente Santa Fe in the first leg delayed by the Colombian earthquake. The series will be decided at the Monumental. Tigre’s campaign ended in Montevideo: Salomón Rodríguez scored twice for City Torque in a 2–1 win, completing a 3–1 aggregate victory.
