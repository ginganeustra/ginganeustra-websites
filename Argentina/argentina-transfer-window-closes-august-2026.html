The window is shut. Argentina’s next roster moves depend on departures.

Liga Profesional clubs can now add only if an overseas sale creates the route.

Argentina La Nuestra · 20 August 2026 · 11:00 ET

TyC Sports reports that the Liga Profesional window has closed. A club that wants another arrival now needs an overseas sale to open the route. A late rumour is not a deal unless the outgoing business happens first.

The confirmed movement remains substantial: Chimy Ávila is officially an Independiente player, Boca’s Marcelo Weigandt has joined Liga de Quito on a one-year loan, and Newell’s loaned Armando Méndez to Guaraní.

What to watch now

La Nuestra will keep official moves, club interest and social-media speculation separate. The immediate question is not simply who a club wants, but whether a foreign departure makes the move possible.

Reporting basis: TyC Sports market tracker, updated 20 August.
