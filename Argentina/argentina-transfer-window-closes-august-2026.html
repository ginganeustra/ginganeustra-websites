The window is shut. Argentina’s next roster moves depend on departures.

TyC Sports reports that Liga Profesional clubs can now add a player only if an overseas sale opens the route. That means a late rumour is not a deal unless the relevant outgoing business happens first.

The confirmed movement is still substantial: Chimy Ávila is officially an Independiente player, Boca’s Marcelo Weigandt has joined Liga de Quito on a one-year loan, and Newell’s loaned Armando Méndez to Guaraní.

La Nuestra will keep official moves, club interest and social-media speculation separate.

Source: TyC Sports market tracker, updated 20 August.
