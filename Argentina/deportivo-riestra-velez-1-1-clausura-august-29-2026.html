Clausura · Round 7 final

Vélez take the point — and the lead.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 18:01 ET / 19:01 ART

Vélez moved top of Clausura Zone A after coming back to draw 1–1 at Deportivo Riestra.

Tomás González, introduced at halftime, headed Riestra in front in the 54th minute from Mariano Bracamonte’s cross. Vélez levelled in the 76th when Facundo Miño turned the ball into his own net.

Manuel Lanzini appeared to complete the comeback late on, but the goal was overturned after video review. The draw left Vélez unbeaten through seven rounds with 13 points, ahead of Instituto on goal difference. Riestra rose to eight points.

The table effect

Vélez have three wins and four draws, with a plus-four goal difference. Instituto also have 13 points but a plus-three differential and have played one match fewer. Gimnasia de Mendoza sit one point behind both.

This report covers only the confirmed final. La Nuestra will add the rest of Saturday’s Clausura results after their final whistles.
