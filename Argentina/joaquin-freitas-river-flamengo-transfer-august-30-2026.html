Freitas is reported close to Flamengo.

Sunday, 30 August 2026

Published 30 August 2026, 18:27 ET / 19:27 ART

River Plate forward Joaquín Freitas is close to a move to Flamengo, according to TyC Sports.

The report says the proposed deal is worth US$17 million including bonuses. Freitas did not play in River’s 3–2 victory at Banfield on Sunday and watched the match from the bench.

No transfer has been announced by either club, so La Nuestra is treating the move as advanced reporting rather than completed business.

If finalized, the deal would move another young River attacker directly into one of South America’s deepest and most expensive squads, while giving River a significant incoming fee during a period of managerial instability.
