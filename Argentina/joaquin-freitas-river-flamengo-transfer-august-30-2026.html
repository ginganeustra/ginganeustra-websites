Flamengo confirm Joaquín Freitas signing from River.

Updated Tuesday, 1 September 2026

Published 30 August 2026, 18:27 ET · Updated 1 September 2026, 11:35 ET / 12:35 ART

Joaquín Freitas is a Flamengo player. The Brazilian club confirmed on Tuesday that the 19-year-old Argentine forward has signed a five-year contract through 2031.

Freitas will wear No. 30. Born in San Fernando, he began at Acassuso and made his professional debut in May 2023 at 16 before joining River Plate in 2024.

The confirmation completes a move first reported during the weekend. TyC Sports reported a package worth US$17 million including bonuses; Flamengo’s announcement did not disclose the transfer fee.

What Flamengo are buying

Freitas arrives at 19 after two years with River. Flamengo highlighted his involvement with Argentina youth selections and said he had been called into preparatory friendlies before the 2026 World Cup.

His departure gives River another significant outgoing transfer while Flamengo deepen a squad already fighting for the Brazilian title. The move also places an Argentine prospect inside one of the continent’s most competitive attacking groups.

Sources: Flamengo’s official confirmation; TyC Sports’ original transfer report.
