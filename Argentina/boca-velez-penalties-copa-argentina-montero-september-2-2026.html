Montero saves twice as Boca eliminate Vélez on penalties.

Thursday, 3 September 2026

Published 3 September 2026, 11:37 ET / 12:37 ART

Boca Juniors reached the Copa Argentina quarter-finals by beating Vélez Sarsfield 4–3 on penalties after a goalless draw at Córdoba’s Mario Alberto Kempes stadium on Wednesday, 2 September.

Álvaro Montero saved the shootout attempts of Elías Gómez and Thiago Silvero. The Colombian goalkeeper’s two stops sent Boca through to face Racing.

Vélez had a chance to settle the tie in added time, but Dilan Godoy struck the post with a penalty. The match finished 0–0 before the shootout.

Lautaro Blanco, Kevin Zenón, Leonel Flores and Carlos Palacios converted for Boca. Tomás Marchiori saved Enner Valencia’s attempt. Joaquín García, Simón Escobar and Rodrigo Aliendro scored for Vélez.

The competition’s official report records Boca’s 13th win in 15 Copa Argentina shootouts. It also notes that the quarter-final pairing reunites the clubs that contested the 2011–12 final, which Boca won.

This was a cup tie, so it does not change the Clausura standings.

Sources: Copa Argentina’s official report; Official completed match sheet; Infobae’s match report.
