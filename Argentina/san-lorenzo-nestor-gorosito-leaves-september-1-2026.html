San Lorenzo · Manager change

Néstor Gorosito leaves San Lorenzo by mutual agreement.

Tuesday, 1 September 2026

Published 1 September 2026, 23:33 ET / 2 September, 00:33 ART

San Lorenzo have confirmed that Néstor “Pipo” Gorosito will not continue as coach of the first team. The club said the parties agreed to end his contract.

The announcement followed Monday’s 1–0 Clausura defeat at Instituto, which left San Lorenzo on seven points from seven matches in Zone A.

What happens next

San Lorenzo did not name a permanent successor. Walter Perazzo, the club’s football co-ordinator, led Tuesday’s training as preparations began for Saturday’s home match against Talleres.

That makes Perazzo the person in charge of the immediate training session, not an announced new manager. The club’s next formal appointment remains to be made.

Sources: San Lorenzo’s official statement; San Lorenzo training update.
