Valdés misses Vélez trip after fresh injury

Published and updated 28 August 2026, 19:15 ET / 20:15 ART

Diego Valdés has not been selected for Vélez’s Clausura match away to Deportivo Riestra after suffering another muscle injury.

The attacking midfielder had recently returned to action in the draw with River Plate, but Olé reports that he has sustained a tear and will miss Saturday’s match. Vélez begin the weekend second in Zone A, one point behind Instituto.

Riestra–Vélez is scheduled for 14:45 ART Saturday. It is the first match of the weekend in the Clausura’s Zone A, making the absence immediately relevant to the leading group.

Sources: Olé report; TyC Sports fixture and table.
