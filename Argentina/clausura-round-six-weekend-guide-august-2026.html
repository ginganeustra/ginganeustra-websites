LA NUESTRA
Weekend guide · Clausura Round 6

Interzonal weekend puts the leaders and giants under the same lens.

The sixth round’s schedule mixes direct local rivalries with fixtures that can redraw the early zone picture.

Saturday offers two useful form tests at 4 p.m. Argentina time: Gimnasia La Plata–Gimnasia de Mendoza and Atlético Tucumán–Instituto. Instituto leads Zone A, so its trip north is about protecting an early advantage rather than merely collecting a sixth-round result.

Independiente–Independiente Rivadavia follows at 6:30 p.m. The evening programme has Newell’s–Banfield and Huracán–Deportivo Riestra at 9 p.m.

Sunday’s attention is in Buenos Aires

River Plate hosts Vélez at 6:15 p.m. and Racing Club hosts Boca Juniors at 8:30 p.m. Neither match needs manufactured hype: River is trying to climb from its slow domestic start, Vélez is near the Zone A lead, and Racing–Boca gives two clubs a high-value interzonal test before the table begins to settle.

Monday completes the round with Tigre–Central Córdoba at 6 p.m., then Lanús–Argentinos Juniors and Talleres–Rosario Central at 8:15 p.m.

Friday results: Aldosivi 1–3 Unión; Estudiantes de Río Cuarto 0–0 San Lorenzo. All remaining listed matches are upcoming.
