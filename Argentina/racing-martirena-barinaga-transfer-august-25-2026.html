Martirena heads out; Barinaga is lined up to replace him.

Racing’s right-back position changed quickly Tuesday night, with Gastón Martirena set for Nacional and Boca’s Juan Barinaga moving toward Avellaneda on loan.

25 August 2026 · evening update

Racing is reshaping its right side late in the window. TyC Sports reported Tuesday evening that Gastón Martirena will leave on loan for Nacional of Uruguay, ending a spell in which he made more than 100 appearances and was part of Racing’s Sudamericana and Recopa-winning teams.

Racing’s response is to move for Juan Barinaga, who has been outside Boca’s main plans. The deal is also expected to be a loan, giving Racing a direct replacement without committing to a major permanent fee.

Martirena had lost his regular starting place in the opening weeks of the Clausura. Racing had also previously considered offers from abroad, including interest from CSKA Moscow, but no sale was completed.

The Neymar wrinkle

Later Tuesday, TyC reported that Neymar personally contacted Martirena as Santos tried to persuade him to change course. Martirena said he had already committed himself to Nacional and chose to honour that agreement.

Why it matters

The move comes with Racing still trying to settle after an uneven Clausura start. A change at fullback is not cosmetic: it alters the balance of a side that has used aggressive width as part of its identity under Diego Milito’s administration.

Boca, meanwhile, clears a player who had fallen down the pecking order while keeping the move temporary. That gives the club flexibility if Barinaga rebuilds value with regular minutes.

Source: TyC Sports, 25 August 2026, evening transfer reports.
