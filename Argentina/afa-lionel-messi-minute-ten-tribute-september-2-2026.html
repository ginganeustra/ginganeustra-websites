Argentine matches to pause for a Messi tribute.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 23:37 ET / 3 September, 00:37 ART

Argentina’s next round of football matches will pause ten minutes into the first half for a minute of applause for Lionel Messi, according to UOL and AS reports citing the Argentine Football Association’s announcement on 2 September.

The timing refers to the No. 10 shirt Messi wore for Argentina. Grounds equipped with screens will also show an institutional video before kickoff, the reports say.

The measure follows Messi’s retirement from international football. UOL reports that the next Clausura round begins on Friday, 4 September, and that the weekend tribute includes the Rosario Central–Newell’s derby.

The announced applause is a recognition within scheduled matches. It should not be confused with confirmation of a separate farewell match or a return by Messi to the national team.

Sources: UOL on AFA’s announcement, 2 September; AS on the tribute, 2 September.
