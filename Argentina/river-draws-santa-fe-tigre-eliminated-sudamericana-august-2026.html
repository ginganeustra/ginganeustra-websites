River leaves Bogotá level. Tigre leaves the competition.

A 0–0 first leg keeps River’s tie open; City Torque’s 2–1 win closes Tigre’s continental campaign.

Argentina La Nuestra news desk · Published 20 August 2026, 15:00 ET

River withstands Santa Fe

River Plate drew 0–0 with Independiente Santa Fe at El Campín in the first leg postponed after the Colombian earthquake. Santa Fe carried more of the attacking threat, but River protected the draw and returns to Buenos Aires with the series level.

Tigre cannot reverse the series

Montevideo City Torque beat Tigre 2–1 at the Estadio Centenario. Salomón Rodríguez scored both goals for the Uruguayan club; Sebastián Moreno scored for Tigre. Combined with City Torque’s 1–0 first-leg win, the result made the aggregate 3–1.

Consequences: Tigre is eliminated. City Torque advances to face the winner of Botafogo–Cienciano. River and Santa Fe still have a second leg to play.
