Argentina · FIFA discipline · appeal confirmed

The AFA will appeal the Paredes and Molina bans.

Its legal manager says the individual sanctions are excessive and will ask FIFA to reduce them.

Argentina La Nuestra news desk · Published 21 August 2026, 11:00 ET · Updated 15:00 ET / 16:00 ART

FIFA has suspended Leandro Paredes for 10 matches and fined him US$90,000 over incidents connected to the World Cup final against Spain. Nahuel Molina received a seven-match suspension and the same fine.

Thiago Almada received a one-match suspension and a US$30,000 fine. Assistant coach Roberto Ayala was suspended for three matches and fined US$30,000.

The AFA sanction

The Argentine Football Association was fined US$321,000 over a wider set of tournament disciplinary issues. At least US$100,000 of that amount is to be directed to an anti-discrimination plan.

The AFA has confirmed that it will appeal. The sanctions remain the operative decisions unless that process changes them.

Issued sanctions:
Paredes — 10 matches, US$90,000
Molina — seven matches, US$90,000
Almada — one match, US$30,000
Ayala — three matches, US$30,000
AFA — US$321,000

The appeal is now confirmed

AFA legal manager Andrés Paton Urich said the association considers the individual sanctions on Paredes, Molina and Ayala excessive and will seek reductions. He said the AFA was notified of six disciplinary decisions on Friday.

An announced appeal is not a successful appeal. Argentina must plan around the issued bans until FIFA or a later tribunal changes them.

What remains unresolved

Reports differ on the precise operation of attendance restrictions attached to the AFA case. Without a consistently available operative ruling, La Nuestra is not presenting that disputed detail as settled fact.

The exact international fixtures affected by the player suspensions can also change through enforcement details or appeal. Availability will be updated only after those points are confirmed.
