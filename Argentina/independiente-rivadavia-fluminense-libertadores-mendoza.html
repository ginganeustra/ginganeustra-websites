Independiente Rivadavia hosts Fluminense with a Libertadores quarter-final place at stake

Independiente Rivadavia faces Fluminense in Mendoza on August 18 at 6 p.m. ET after a 0–0 Copa Libertadores first leg.

Published and updated 17 August 2026, 22:02 ET

By Argentina La Nuestra News Desk · 17 August 2026 · 22:02 ET

Independiente Rivadavia will host Fluminense in Mendoza on Tuesday with their Copa Libertadores round-of-16 tie still level at 0–0 after the first match in Rio de Janeiro.

CONMEBOL lists kickoff for 7 p.m. local time at Estadio Malvinas Argentinas, or 6 p.m. Eastern. A win sends the Mendoza club into the quarter-finals; another draw would take the tie to penalties.

The domestic buildup was less encouraging. Independiente Rivadavia lost 2–0 to Belgrano on Saturday and slipped to 10th in Clausura Zone B with seven points. Fluminense, by contrast, arrives after a 3–2 comeback win over Brasileirão leader Palmeiras.

The match is also a particularly sharp example of the geographic story in La Nuestra’s recurring Spotlight: a club from Mendoza, far from Buenos Aires, has the opportunity to eliminate one of Brazil’s established continental powers on its own ground.
