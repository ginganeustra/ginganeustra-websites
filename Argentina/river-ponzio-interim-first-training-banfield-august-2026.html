Ponzio’s first day starts the River reset.

Published 28 August 2026, 18:55 ET · Updated 29 August 2026, 09:15 ET

Leonardo Ponzio has led his first River Plate first-team training session after taking interim charge following Eduardo Coudet’s departure.

The former River captain is working with assistants Marcelo Escudero and Marcelo Barovero. His first match in charge is scheduled for Sunday afternoon at Banfield in Clausura Round 7.

Ponzio inherits a team 14th in Zone B with four points from six matches. The appointment is interim; La Nuestra will not treat reports about River’s longer-term search as settled until the club confirms its decision.

Sources: OneFootball report; El País Argentina, 27 August 2026.
