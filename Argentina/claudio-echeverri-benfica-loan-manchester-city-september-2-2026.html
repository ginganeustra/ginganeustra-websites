Claudio Echeverri joins Benfica on loan.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 23:37 ET / 3 September, 00:37 ART

Benfica have confirmed the signing of Claudio Echeverri on loan from Manchester City until the end of the 2026–27 season, with an option to buy.

The Portuguese club presented the 20-year-old Argentine attacking midfielder on 2 September. Benfica’s announcement and presentation material establish the move as completed business, rather than an agreement still being negotiated.

The confirmed arrangement is a loan with a purchase option, not an immediate permanent transfer. Benfica can assess the midfielder over the coming season before deciding whether to buy him.

Echeverri’s arrival follows another significant move involving an Argentine at City: Enzo Fernández’s confirmed transfer from Chelsea. The two deals send the players in different directions and have different contractual structures.

Sources: Benfica’s official announcement; Benfica’s presentation; Record’s confirmation.
