Kily González takes Inter Miami job, pending work permits.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 09:15 ET / 10:15 ART

Inter Miami has reached an agreement for Cristian “Kily” González to become its first-team head coach, putting another prominent Argentine manager alongside Lionel Messi in Major League Soccer.

The club said González will formally take charge after receiving the required U.S. work permits. Guillermo Hoyos will remain in interim control until González and his staff arrive, so the appointment does not automatically put the new coach on the touchline for Saturday evening’s match against CF Montréal.

González, 52, won 56 caps for Argentina between 1995 and 2005. His club career included Rosario Central, Boca Juniors, Valencia and Inter Milan. As a manager, he has worked at Rosario Central, Unión and Platense.

The appointment follows Javier Mascherano’s April departure. It also places González in charge of an Inter Miami squad built around Argentina captain Messi and a substantial Argentine influence.

The immediate distinction

Inter Miami’s wording is precise: the agreement is complete, but González cannot begin official duties until the permits are issued. Hoyos therefore remains the responsible coach for the transition period.

Inter Miami entered the weekend second in the MLS Eastern Conference with 39 points from 21 matches. Its official match preview lists Saturday’s home fixture against Montréal for 7:30 p.m. ET.
