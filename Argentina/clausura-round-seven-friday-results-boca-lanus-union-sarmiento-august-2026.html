Belmonte settles Boca–Lanús; Unión score four.

Friday, 28 August 2026

Published 28 August 2026, 23:34 ET / 00:34 ART

Boca 1–0 Lanús
Tomás Belmonte 90+3

Boca Juniors opened Round 7 with a 1–0 home win over Lanús, settled by Tomás Belmonte in the third minute of added time. The result moved Boca to 10 points from seven matches in Zone A; Lanús remain on seven points after seven.

Unión 4–1 Sarmiento
Cristian Tarragona 18, 61; Víctor Malcorra 74; Eric Ramírez 84 — Julián Mavilla 79

In the interzonal match, Unión beat Sarmiento 4–1. Tarragona scored twice before Malcorra and Ramírez added late goals; Mavilla scored Sarmiento’s consolation. Unión reached 10 points from seven games, while Sarmiento stayed on 12 in Zone B.

What the table says

In Zone A, Boca sit eighth on 10 points, behind Unión and Newell’s on the published tie-break order. In Zone B, Sarmiento remain second behind Argentinos Juniors, with the rest of the round still to be played.
