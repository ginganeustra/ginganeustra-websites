Rosario Central · Copa Libertadores

Central gets the extra man. Corinthians gets the goal.

Breno Bidon’s 79th-minute finish ends Rosario Central’s first Libertadores knockout run since 2016.

Argentina La Nuestra news desk · Published 20 August 2026, 23:00 ET / 21 August, 00:00 ART

Rosario Central was eliminated after a 1–0 defeat at Corinthians. The first leg had ended 0–0, so Breno Bidon’s late goal decided both match and tie.

The opening Central needed

Raniele was dismissed in the 57th minute, giving Central more than half an hour with a numerical advantage. Ángel Di María and his teammates generated pressure, but Hugo Souza and the home defence held.

Memphis changes the match

Memphis Depay returned from the bench after renewing his contract. In the 79th minute he delivered the cross that Bidon converted.

What comes next: Corinthians advances to face Estudiantes. Rosario Central returns to a domestic campaign in which it sits third in Zone B after five rounds.
