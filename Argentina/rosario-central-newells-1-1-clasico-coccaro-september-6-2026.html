Cóccaro rescues Newell’s point in Rosario clásico

Sunday, 6 September 2026

Published 6 September 2026, 18:00 ET / 19:00 ART

Archive photograph: Gigante de Arroyito, Pablo D. Flores / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.5.

Matías Cóccaro scored deep in stoppage time to earn Newell’s a 1–1 draw against Rosario Central in the Rosario clásico.

Gastón Ávila put Central ahead with a left-footed strike just before halftime. Cóccaro’s late finish was initially ruled offside, but the goal was awarded after a VAR review.

The point takes Newell’s to 12 points in Zone A and Rosario Central to 12 in Zone B. The match also paused in the tenth minute for the AFA’s announced tribute to Lionel Messi; Central supporters followed the applause by chanting for Ángel Di María.

Later Sunday matches that were not final at publication time are excluded. Racing–Atlético Tucumán remains an upcoming fixture; Round 8 concludes Monday with Barracas Central–Argentinos Juniors and Unión–Instituto.
