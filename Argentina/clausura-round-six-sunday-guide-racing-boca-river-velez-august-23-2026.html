LA NUESTRA
Clausura · Round 6 · Sunday, Aug. 23

A clásico and an unbeaten test define Sunday.

Racing–Boca closes the night in Avellaneda after River tries to build on its first Clausura win against unbeaten Vélez.

Published Aug. 23, 2026 · 02:00 ET / 03:00 ART

Round 6 is the interzonal round, and Sunday carries two of its biggest fixtures. Boca and Racing both enter the clásico with only one Clausura win, while River is trying to turn last week’s 2–0 victory over Argentinos Juniors into an actual run of form.

Sarmiento–Estudiantes LP · 14:45 ART

Estudiantes has ground to make up in Zone A and Sarmiento has been one of the stronger early teams in Zone B. It is an important early test of whether Estudiantes can translate its continental momentum into league points.

Barracas Central–Platense · 14:45 ART

Barracas begins the day inside the Zone B qualification places. Platense is below the line in Zone A and needs a result to stop the early gap from growing.

Belgrano–Defensa y Justicia · 17:00 ART

Belgrano has started strongly in Zone B; Defensa remains in the Zone A playoff picture. This is one of Sunday’s clearest direct comparisons between two teams currently above their respective cut lines.

River–Vélez · 19:15 ART

River finally won its first Clausura match last weekend, beating Argentinos Juniors 2–0. Vélez comes to the Monumental unbeaten through five league matches. TyC Sports reports Sebastián Driussi has been medically cleared and available. Darío Herrera is the referee.

Racing–Boca · 21:30 ART

The night finishes with the clásico at the Cilindro. Both clubs have only one league win and both need points to move toward the top-eight qualification line. Racing comes from a Copa Argentina win over Belgrano; Boca arrives after advancing in the Sudamericana. Nicolás Ramírez is the referee.

Monday completes the round with Tigre–Central Córdoba, Talleres–Rosario Central and Lanús–Argentinos Juniors.

Fixture and match context verified against TyC Sports’ Round 6 schedule and match previews. A correction has also been applied across the front page: Newell’s beat Banfield 2–0 on Saturday, not 2–1.
