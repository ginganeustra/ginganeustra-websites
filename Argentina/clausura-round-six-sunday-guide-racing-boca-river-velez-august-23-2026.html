Clausura · Round 6 · live Sunday

Sarmiento and Barracas lead before the heavyweight night.

Both early matches stand 1–0 in the second half. River–Vélez and Racing–Boca remain ahead.

Published Aug. 23, 2026 · Updated 15:00 ET / 16:00 ART

Round 6 is the interzonal round. The first Sunday matches are in their second halves: Sarmiento leads Estudiantes de La Plata 1–0 and Barracas Central leads Platense 1–0. Those are live scores, not final results.

If both leads hold, Sarmiento and Barracas will each reach 12 points in Zone B. The evening then brings River–Vélez and Racing–Boca.

Live: Sarmiento 1–0 Estudiantes LP

Sarmiento is defending a second-half lead that would lift it to 12 points in Zone B. Estudiantes needs a response to prevent a fourth Clausura defeat.

Live: Barracas Central 1–0 Platense

Barracas is also protecting a second-half lead. A victory would move it to 12 points and strengthen its position inside the Zone B qualification places.

Belgrano–Defensa y Justicia · 17:00 ART

Belgrano has started strongly in Zone B; Defensa remains in the Zone A playoff picture. This is one of Sunday’s clearest direct comparisons between two teams currently above their respective cut lines.

River–Vélez · 19:15 ART

River finally won its first Clausura match last weekend, beating Argentinos Juniors 2–0. Vélez comes to the Monumental unbeaten through five league matches. TyC Sports reports Sebastián Driussi has been medically cleared and available. Darío Herrera is the referee.

Racing–Boca · 21:30 ART

The night finishes with the clásico at the Cilindro. Both clubs have only one league win and both need points to move toward the top-eight qualification line. Racing comes from a Copa Argentina win over Belgrano; Boca arrives after advancing in the Sudamericana. Nicolás Ramírez is the referee.

Monday completes the round with Tigre–Central Córdoba, Talleres–Rosario Central and Lanús–Argentinos Juniors.

Live scores and the complete schedule verified at 16:00 ART through the Liga Profesional de Fútbol. Saturday correction: Newell’s beat Banfield 2–1.
