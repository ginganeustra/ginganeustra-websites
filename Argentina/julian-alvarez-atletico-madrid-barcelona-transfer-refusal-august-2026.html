Atlético close Barcelona’s door on Julián Álvarez.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 10:55 ET / 11:55 ART

Atlético Madrid’s board has unanimously backed the club’s decision not to negotiate Julián Álvarez’s transfer to Barcelona.

The official statement says Atlético remains open to ordinary market talks when they are conducted professionally and respectfully, but accuses Barcelona of failing that standard. On that basis, the directors say there is no consideration of an Álvarez move to the Catalan club.

Atlético also says it expects the Argentina forward to understand the decision and remain focused on the team. Reuters reported that the intervention followed public expressions of interest from Barcelona president Joan Laporta.

What the decision means

The statement is categorical about Barcelona. It is not an announcement of a new contract, nor does it say Álvarez can never leave Atlético for another destination. La Nuestra is therefore reporting the precise decision: Atlético will not negotiate this transfer with Barcelona.

The 2022 World Cup winner joined Atlético from Manchester City in 2024. His status has become one of the summer market’s major stories, but no new move is confirmed.

Sources: Atlético Madrid board statement; Reuters report, 27 August 2026.
