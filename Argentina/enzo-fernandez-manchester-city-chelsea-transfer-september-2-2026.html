Enzo Fernández joins Manchester City through 2031.

Wednesday, 2 September 2026

Published 2 September 2026, 11:36 ET / 12:36 ART

Manchester City have completed the signing of Enzo Fernández from Chelsea on a five-year contract. The Argentina midfielder’s deal runs until the summer of 2031.

The move reunites Fernández with Enzo Maresca, his former Chelsea coach. Reuters reports a £125-million fee; that figure is a reported price, not a sum disclosed in City’s announcement.

From River and Defensa to another major move

Fernández, 25, developed at River Plate and gained important first-team experience on loan at Defensa y Justicia before moving to Benfica and then Chelsea.

City’s profile credits him with 15 goals and seven assists in 54 appearances for Chelsea last season. His international honours include Argentina’s 2022 World Cup and 2024 Copa América titles.

The signing is confirmed, ending the uncertainty over his club destination. The announcement does not set a debut date.

Sources: Manchester City’s official announcement; Reuters on the completed transfer and reported fee.
