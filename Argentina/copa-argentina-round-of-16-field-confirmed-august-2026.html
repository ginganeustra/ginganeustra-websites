Domestic cup · official agenda

Copa Argentina’s last 16 is now a complete field.

The AFA has listed the eight ties, giving the domestic calendar another knockout thread alongside the Clausura.

The listed Round of 16 includes Deportivo Riestra–Gimnasia La Plata, Banfield–Midland, Racing–Belgrano, Vélez–Boca, Aldosivi–Independiente Rivadavia, Atlético Tucumán–Independiente, Platense–Instituto and Estudiantes–Barracas Central.

Those pairings offer a second immediate pressure point for several league clubs. The Copa Argentina is single-elimination: a draw is decided by penalties, according to the competition regulation cited in the AFA agenda.
