Clausura · Round 7 finals

Gimnasia steal it in Rosario.

Saturday, 29 August 2026

Published 29 August 2026, 23:01 ET / 00:01 ART

Ignacio Miramón scored in the 88th minute to give Gimnasia a 2–1 win over Rosario Central at the Gigante de Arroyito.

Gimnasia went ahead through a Cristian Ledesma own goal in the 59th minute. Jaminton Campaz equalized for Central in the 86th, only for Miramón to restore the visitors’ lead two minutes later.

The result took Gimnasia to 12 points in Zone B, level with second-place Sarmiento. Rosario Central remain on 11 and fell to sixth as the Saturday table settled.

Three draws complete Saturday

Huracán and Estudiantes de Río Cuarto drew 1–1 after exchanging early penalties: Ignacio Pussetto scored in the third minute and Ibrahim Hesar answered in the 18th.

Talleres and Central Córdoba finished 0–0 in Córdoba. Atlético Tucumán and Belgrano also produced a goalless draw. Earlier, Deportivo Riestra and Vélez drew 1–1, a result that left Vélez top of Zone A on goal difference.

All five Saturday Clausura matches are final. Sunday begins with Banfield–River at 15:00 ART.
