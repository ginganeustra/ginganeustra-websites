Clausura Round 8 continues with five Saturday matches after Friday’s three completed games. The day begins with Aldosivi–Banfield at 13:30 ART and Gimnasia LP–Tigre at 14:30.

Boca start the day on 10 points in Zone A and visit a Gimnasia Mendoza side sitting second on 15. Vélez, fourth on 13, close the Saturday schedule against Estudiantes.

Sunday’s programme is led by Rosario Central–Newell’s at 14:45 ART. River visit Independiente Rivadavia at 19:15, while Racing host Atlético Tucumán at 21:30.
