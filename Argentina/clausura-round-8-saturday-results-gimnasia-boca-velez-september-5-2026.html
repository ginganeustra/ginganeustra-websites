Gimnasia Mendoza rescue point; Vélez join leaders

Saturday, 5 September 2026

Published 5 September 2026, 22:57 ET / 23:57 ART

Archive photograph: José Amalfitani stadium, Gonzabar77 / Wikimedia Commons, CC0 1.0.

Esteban Fernández scored in the third minute of stoppage time to earn Gimnasia Mendoza a 2–2 draw with Boca. Alan Velasco and Kevin Zenón scored for Boca, but the late equalizer moved Gimnasia to 16 points in Zone A.

Vélez draw level

Vélez beat Estudiantes 1–0 at the José Amalfitani and also reached 16 points. Gimnasia Mendoza lead the zone on goal difference, with Vélez second and Instituto third before Sunday’s matches.

Gimnasia La Plata beat Tigre 2–1 to move to 15 points in Zone B, level with Sarmiento and one behind Argentinos Juniors. San Lorenzo defeated Talleres 1–0 and climbed to 10 points in Zone A.

Aldosivi finally win

Aldosivi beat Banfield 3–1 for their first victory of 2026. The result moved the Mar del Plata club above Racing at the bottom of Zone B.
