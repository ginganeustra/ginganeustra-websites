Sampaoli says he will stay and fight through the pressure.

Jorge Sampaoli rejected resignation talk after Talleres surrendered a two-goal lead against Rosario Central and remained last in Clausura Zone A.

Published 26 August 2026 · 12:00 ET / 13:00 ART

Jorge Sampaoli says he has not resigned and intends to remain Talleres coach despite a difficult start to the Clausura and mounting frustration in Córdoba.

“I did not submit my resignation. Otherwise I would not be here,” Sampaoli said after Monday’s 2–2 draw with Rosario Central. Talleres had led 2–0 before Ángel Di María converted two penalties for the visitors.

The result left Talleres bottom of Zone A with four points from six matches. Supporters voiced their anger at the Mario Alberto Kempes, extending the pressure that had followed defeats by Lanús and Gimnasia Mendoza.

The message from the coach

Sampaoli said his focus is on improving the team and described persistence as central to his career. He also acknowledged that Talleres has not developed the intensity or control he wants and that he is adapting his ideas to the players available.

Why the decision matters

His statement removes the immediate uncertainty over the technical staff, but it does not remove the sporting urgency. Talleres has one win through six Clausura rounds and remains outside the eight Zone A playoff places.
