Clausura · Round 7 finals

Gimnasia Mendoza go top; Racing beaten.

Sunday, 30 August 2026

Published 30 August 2026, 18:27 ET · Updated 23:14 ET / 31 August, 00:14 ART

River Plate beat Banfield 3–2 in Leonardo Ponzio’s first match as interim coach, ending a difficult week with their second Clausura victory.

Sebastián Driussi opened the scoring in the 32nd minute, Thiago Almada converted a penalty nine minutes later and Ángel Correa made it 3–1 in the 69th. Bruno Sepúlveda scored twice for Banfield, including an 84th-minute goal that made the finish tense.

The win moved River to seven points from seven matches. Ponzio confirmed after the match that his role remains an interim appointment.

Argentinos come back to beat Aldosivi

Argentinos Juniors recovered from a first-half deficit to defeat Aldosivi 2–1. Andrés Vombergar put Aldosivi ahead, but Alan Lescano equalized in the 54th minute and Gastón Verón completed the comeback 12 minutes later.

Argentinos move to 16 points from seven matches at the top of Zone B. Aldosivi remain on two points.

Gimnasia Mendoza take over Zone A

Gimnasia Mendoza won 3–0 at Independiente. Esteban Fernández opened the scoring in the 41st minute before Facundo Lencioni struck twice early in the second half.

The promoted side move to 15 points and first place in Zone A. Independiente stay on 10 points after suffering their first Clausura defeat under Jadson Viera.

Racing beaten in Mendoza

Independiente Rivadavia defeated Racing 3–1 at the Bautista Gargantini. Matko Miljevic, Fabrizio Sartori and Alex Arce scored for the home side.

The victory takes Independiente Rivadavia to 11 points and seventh in Zone B. Racing remain 14th with five points from seven matches.
